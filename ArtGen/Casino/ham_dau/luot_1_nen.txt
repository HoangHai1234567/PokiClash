WHAT IT IS
The background of a game screen: a secret UNDERGROUND FIGHTING PIT in the cellar below a small gambling island's casino. Pets fight here while the house bookie watches from his booth. Mood: rowdy, cheeky, a bit shady, never scary. Set design in the spirit of early-1930s rubber-hose cartoons: wonky bouncy architecture, chunky exaggerated props, a crowd of simple cartoon onlookers. Drawn with the technique of image 1 (flat crayon hatching + brown ink line).

The picture is an EMPTY STAGE: game characters are added later by the game. So the bookie's booth must be EMPTY and the pit floor must be EMPTY.

LAYOUT (y measured from the top of the picture, 0 % = top edge, 100 % = bottom edge)
1. 0 %-12 %: ceiling. A low vaulted cellar ceiling of big rounded stones and brick with two heavy crooked wooden beams. From the beams hang a string of card-suit pennants (spade, heart, diamond, club shapes only) and four round paper lanterns like the ones in image 1.
2. 12 %-33 %: back wall, centre: THE BOOKIE'S BOOTH. A wooden balcony box like a boxing announcer's stand or a small theatre box, centred, about 56 % of the picture width. It has a striped red-and-cream awning with a gold scalloped fringe on top and two chunky posts. The inside of the booth is a plain flat WARM CREAM-YELLOW back wall (so that a black character placed there later reads clearly). Nobody is inside. Nothing stands on the counter.
3. At exactly 33 %: the TOP EDGE OF THE BOOTH COUNTER, one clean, perfectly straight, horizontal brown ink line across the full width of the booth. The counter top is a narrow strip of green felt edged with brass.
4. 33 %-46 %: the FRONT PANEL of the booth: solid wooden planks, a row of big poker-chip medallions along its upper part, and in its centre one large BLANK wooden signboard hanging from two short chains (plain light wood, about 46 % of the picture width and 7 % of its height, completely empty: the game writes on it). Below the panel, between two stone pillars, a low dark iron-barred gate (closed) where fighters enter.
5. 12 %-46 %, left and right of the booth: steep wooden bleachers packed with a CROWD OF ONLOOKERS: simple dark navy-plum silhouette blobs with round white pie-cut cartoon eyes, some with little hats, some waving tiny flags or holding poker chips and cards. They are background shapes, low contrast, all looking down at the pit. Among them: a slate betting board covered with chalk tally marks and scribbles (marks only, nothing readable), a stack of barrels, a pile of treasure chests.
6. 46 %-82 %: THE PIT. A big round sunken arena of pale ochre sand seen from a slightly raised front view, an ellipse nearly as wide as the picture. Behind it a low curved wall of rough planks and stones with a thick rope along the top and a few poker chips nailed on as decoration. The sand floor is CLEAN AND EMPTY: flat pale ochre crayon hatching, only one faint chalk ring drawn on it and three or four tiny pebbles near the rim. No characters, no props, no marks in the middle.
7. 82 %-100 %: foreground, calm and dark because game buttons sit on top of it: the near rim of the pit (a curved plank barrier with a rope) and, in front of it, the backs of the heads and shoulders of the front-row onlookers as big flat dark navy-plum silhouettes, a few raised fists and one raised hand holding playing cards. Keep this band simple and low in detail.

COLOURS
Walls and ceiling: deep plum, indigo and slate stone. Wood: warm reddish browns. Sand: pale ochre. Accents from the casino: wine red, gold, emerald green felt, a little teal. Lanterns: flat warm yellow-orange. Limited vintage palette, strong contrast between the pale pit floor and the dark surroundings.

RULES (strict)
- Flat colours only: every area one even colour with crayon hatching as texture. NO lighting: no shadows, no cast shadows, no light cones, no glow or halo around the lanterns, no gradients, no shiny highlights, no fog.
- Dark brown ink outlines, slightly wobbly, same weight as image 1.
- NO text, letters, numbers or readable signs anywhere. The hanging signboard stays blank.
- No main characters: the booth is empty, the pit floor is empty.
- Do not draw any user interface, frame or logo.
