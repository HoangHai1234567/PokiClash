DRAWING STYLE (the game's own look: copy the line and the colouring of the game's hand-drawn map): drawn by hand ON PAPER by a professional storybook illustrator: outlines in dark-brown ink pen (#2B2118), fairly even weight, slightly wobbly like a real pen; every area coloured by hand with COLOURED PENCILS and WAX CRAYONS, the strokes clearly visible as loose diagonal hatching, which gives the soft crayon-on-paper texture; clean and appealing, neither a crude kid scribble nor overly detailed.
FLAT COLOUR, NO LIGHT, NO SHADOW: every area is one even colour; the pencil and crayon strokes are texture only, they do not model light. No shading, no darker side, no shadows of any kind (no cast shadow, no shadow under the figure, on the ground or on the background), no white highlights or shine spots, no gloss, no glow, no rim light, no gradients, no 3D look. Plain white background around the figure: no paper colour or paper grain outside it, no white border or sticker outline around the figure or its parts (the game adds that itself).
