Concept art for a vertical mobile creature-collecting game: the player's HOME ISLAND fully zoomed out, the whole base visible, mid-game.

KEEP FROM IMAGE 2 (an approved earlier concept of this home): the drawing style, the dense forest look, the building designs (red-roofed main house, fenced vegetable fields, apple orchard, fishing pond with jetty, sawmill, stone forge, kitchen cottage, hot spring, ball tree, storage barn, mine with crystal ore), the environments (waterfall hills, stream, beaches), the signposts and decorations. CHANGE two things: the camera and the size of the island.

CAMERA (most important): strict isometric projection like a classic mobile base-building game (2:1 dimetric). The ground is an invisible diamond grid whose lines run in the two diagonal directions, about 27 degrees from horizontal (each cell is twice as wide as it is tall). Every lot edge, building footprint, field, fence, path tile and the overall shoreline follow these two diagonal directions. Every building, tree clump and prop sits on a diamond-shaped footprint with one corner pointing straight down toward the viewer, so we always see its roof plus its left wall and its right wall, all at the same angle; vertical edges stay perfectly vertical. No perspective: parallel lines stay parallel and far objects are the same size as near ones. Every object uses exactly the same angle.

SIZE: the island is very large and wide: one big diamond-shaped land aligned to the grid, made of 6 by 6 forest lots. The settlement covers only about a quarter of it; most of the land is still dense forest waiting to be cleared.

LAYOUT: follow image 4, which is only a rough layout sketch (not a style reference): its boxes only show footprint, angle and relative size, and its thin white lines only show lot borders. Do NOT draw numbers, letters, grid lines or lot borders.
- L: locked forest lots. Each lot is a diamond block of land completely covered by tall dense trees (pines and round broadleaf trees, a few mossy boulders, the ground hidden). Forest blocks follow the grid diagonals, so the cleared area has straight diagonal edges with stepped corners. Small wooden signposts with a padlock stand at the front of the locked lots next to the settlement.
- S: lots ready to clear: the same forest with a signpost carrying a round axe icon.
- C: a lot being cleared: stumps, felled logs, flying leaves; creature B chops with an axe.
- H: grassy plateaus with short hatched brown cliff faces and pine trees; a waterfall from them feeds a stream that runs through a forest lake (W) down to the fishing pond (5).
- W: lakes inside forest lots.
- Light green: the cleared meadow of the settlement.
- Beyond the two upper edges of the land: a band of higher green hills with brown cliffs, and along the very top of the picture a far landscape strip of distant hills and a river. Beyond the two lower edges: a sandy beach band following the diagonals, then the sea. The shoreline is irregular in detail but runs along the diagonals overall.
- D: a wooden dock at the bottom corner of the land with a moored sailboat.

BUILDINGS (numbers in image 4; footprints and relative sizes as in the sketch): 1 main house (the biggest); 2 and 3 fenced vegetable fields (creature A hoes in field 2); 4 apple orchard; 5 fishing pond with a jetty (creature C fishing); 6 sawmill with stacked logs; 7 stone well with small irrigation ditches to the fields; 8 kitchen cottage with a stone oven (creature E stirs a pot); 9 stone forge with an anvil (creature F hammering); 10 hot spring pool (creature G soaking); 11 ball tree whose fruits are white balls with one grey stripe; 12 storage barn; 13 mine on a grey rock outcrop with crystal ore, a timber entrance and a minecart (creature D with a pickaxe). A dirt path of square grid tiles runs from the dock to the main house; a small plaza with a stone sea-dragon statue, flower beds, fences along the diagonals, lanterns, a bench.

Creatures exactly as in image 3 (same design, colours and silhouette), small (about one grid cell), each once. No people.

STYLE: hand-drawn on cream paper, wobbly dark-brown ink outlines, coloured pencil and wax crayon hatching, one flat colour per area, chunky cute doodles (image 1, image 2). No drop shadows, no cast shadows on the ground, no glossy highlights, no glow, no gradients, no 3D render. Sea flat blue with pencil hatching and a white foam line along the shore.
