Concept mockup of ONE SCREEN of a vertical mobile game (9:16 portrait) at normal play zoom: the player is at HOME, looking at one part of a large home island, with the game UI on top. The land clearly continues beyond the left, right and top edges of the screen.

KEEP: the building designs, the forest look and the drawing style of image 2, and the UI design of image 3. CHANGE: the camera.

CAMERA (most important): strict isometric projection like a classic mobile base-building game (2:1 dimetric). The ground is an invisible diamond grid whose lines run in the two diagonal directions, about 27 degrees from horizontal (each cell is twice as wide as it is tall). Every lot edge, building footprint, field, fence, path tile and the shoreline follow these two diagonal directions. Every building, tree clump and prop sits on a diamond-shaped footprint with one corner pointing straight down toward the viewer, so we always see its roof plus its left wall and its right wall, all at the same angle; vertical edges stay perfectly vertical. No perspective. Zoomed in: one grid cell is about one tenth of the screen width, the main house is about half the screen width.

LAYOUT: follow image 5, which is only a rough layout sketch (not a style reference): its boxes only show footprint, angle and relative size. Do NOT draw numbers, letters or grid lines.

STYLE: hand-drawn on cream paper, wobbly dark-brown ink outlines, coloured pencil and wax crayon hatching, one flat colour per area, chunky cute doodles (image 1, image 2). No drop shadows, no cast shadows on the ground, no glossy highlights, no glow, no gradients, no 3D render.
