Concept art for a mobile creature-collecting game: an adventure world map of ONE large, wide forest island (much wider than tall), seen from above at a gentle three-quarter angle, shown whole, divided into ten regions, each with its own environment.

STYLE: hand-drawn on cream paper, wobbly dark-brown ink outlines, coloured pencil and wax crayon hatching, one flat colour per area, chunky cute doodles, rich detail like image 1. No drop shadows, no cast shadows on the ground, no glossy highlights, no glow, no light rays, no gradients, no 3D render.

LAYOUT: follow image 3, a rough layout sketch (not a style reference). Each coloured patch is one region; borders blend naturally through terrain (cliffs, rivers, tree lines, rock ridges). The island outline is irregular and organic, with small bays and rocky points, not an oval.
THE ROAD: there is ONLY ONE pale sand road, no forks, no side roads, no shortcuts. It starts at the wooden pier at the bottom and visits the habitats strictly in order 1, 5, 10, 15, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60, 70: first counter-clockwise around the outer regions, then it turns inward and spirals to the giant tree at the heart of the island. The road must go RIGHT INTO the heart of every region and arrive at the front of its habitat, then leave it on the other side; it never skirts around a region and never crosses itself. It winds naturally like a real country trail: irregular bends, small switchbacks up slopes, a wooden bridge over a stream, stone steps up a cliff, a rope bridge in the jungle, stepping stones through the meadow. White dots are minor stages: small red pennant flags on short poles right beside the road. Do NOT draw numbers, letters, grid lines or diamond outlines.

REGIONS AND HABITATS (each habitat is a round raised creature enclosure with a low stone border, decorated for its region; the creature of the same number from image 2 stands inside, same design, colours and silhouette as image 2, confident ready stance, about one third of the habitat width; 50, 60, 70 are bigger):
1 MOSSY SHORE at the bottom, by the pier: mossy boulders, ferns, little tide pools, driftwood; mossy stone sparring ring (spiky cactus creature).
5 BRIGHT FLOWER MEADOW in the south-east: rolling meadow full of big colourful flowers and sunflowers, butterflies, beehives; ring of flower beds (round green sprout creature).
10 MUSHROOM HILLS on the east coast: giant red-capped and orange mushrooms, small mushroom huts; habitat on top of a giant flat mushroom cap (floating stone-ring creature).
15 VINE JUNGLE in the north-east: dense jungle trees, hanging vines, big leaves, a rope bridge; habitat wrapped in thick vines (wooden deer with autumn leaves).
20 WIND FANG CAVE at the top: grey-teal rocky hills, a cave mouth framed by huge fang-shaped rocks, curly wind lines; habitat at the cave mouth (leafy elephant).
30 LEAF VALLEY in the north-west: a valley of maple trees in orange and yellow, piles of fallen leaves; habitat ring of fallen leaves (round stone golem with a green crystal).
40 DRAGON CLAW CLIFFS on the west coast: dark brown cliffs with tall rock spires shaped like giant claws; habitat on a cliff ledge between the claws (leaf-armoured fighter).
50 JADE BAMBOO FOREST in the south-west: jade-green bamboo groves, stone lanterns, a little stream with a wooden bridge; habitat in a bamboo clearing (monkey general with a staff).
60 GREEN CRYSTAL CAVE on an inner hill west of the centre: a hill with a cave full of big bright green crystal clusters; habitat among the crystals (wooden beast with crystals).
70 THOUSAND-YEAR SACRED TREE at the heart of the island, the final region: one gigantic ancient tree with a huge canopy and massive roots, a small shrine and stone lanterns at its foot; the biggest habitat sits between the roots (dark green dragon).

Around the island: open sea with white foam lines along the shore, a sailing boat, seagulls, two or three clouds drifting over the edges.
