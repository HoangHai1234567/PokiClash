Concept art for a mobile creature-collecting game: an adventure world map of ONE large, wide island (much wider than tall), seen from above at a gentle three-quarter angle, shown whole. The island is divided into ten distinct regions, each with its own environment, like the regions of a hand-painted campaign map in a strategy game.

STYLE: exactly the drawing style of image 1 and image 2: hand-drawn on cream paper, wobbly dark-brown ink outlines, coloured pencil and wax crayon hatching, one flat colour per area, chunky cute doodles with clear silhouettes. No drop shadows, no cast shadows on the ground, no glossy highlights, no glow, no gradients, no 3D render.

LAYOUT: follow image 4, which is only a rough layout sketch (not a style reference). Each coloured patch is one region; the region borders blend naturally through terrain (cliffs, rivers, tree lines, rock ridges), never hard lines. Pale sand roads form a NETWORK linking neighbouring regions, with forks and loops, so the player can travel many ways; there is no single trail. A wooden pier at the bottom middle is where the journey starts. White dots are minor stages: draw each as a small pennant flag on a short pole beside a road or a little side path inside its region. Dashed diamonds marked R are empty plots: flat grass clearings with a few bushes and rocks, nothing built. Do NOT draw numbers, letters, grid lines or diamond outlines.

HABITATS: in the heart of each region sits that region's guardian habitat: a raised creature enclosure on its own diamond-shaped platform with a low themed border (stones, shells, coral, ice blocks...), decorated to match the region, with the creature of the same number from image 3 standing inside it (same design, colours and silhouette as image 3, in a confident ready stance, about one third of the habitat width). Habitats 50, 60 and 70 are bigger.

20: SHIPWRECK BAY region: dark cove, broken masts, barrels on the beach; habitat = half-sunken wooden shipwreck (red octopus).

40: WHALE BAY region: blue-grey cliffs around a bay, giant whale bones on the shore; habitat = giant whale-bone arch over a pool (blue whale).
50: ABYSS CAVES region: dark navy and purple rocks, deep crevices, small glowing blue lantern mushrooms; habitat = dark sea-cave mouth (horned blue serpent).
60: GLACIER region in the top-left corner: snowfield, ice cliffs, frozen pools; habitat = frozen lagoon with ice floes and icicle rocks (winged blue sea dragon).
70: DRAGON TEMPLE region at the top centre, the final region: ancient ruined temple with broken columns beside a big round whirlpool bay; habitat = the temple steps with the young sea dragon (the biggest habitat).
Difficulty rises from the sunny beach at the bottom to the frozen and dark regions at the top.

Around the island: open sea with white foam lines along the shore, a sailing boat, seagulls, two or three clouds drifting over the edges.
No text, no UI, no people.
