Concept art for a mobile creature-collecting game: an adventure world map of ONE large, wide island seen from above at a gentle three-quarter angle, shown whole.

KEEP THE LOOK OF IMAGE 4 (an approved concept of this same island): same drawing style, same kind of regions, same kind of creature habitats, same props, same sea. Only the arrangement and the road change, following image 3.

STYLE: hand-drawn on cream paper, wobbly dark-brown ink outlines, coloured pencil and wax crayon hatching, one flat colour per area, chunky cute doodles (image 1). No drop shadows, no cast shadows on the ground, no glossy highlights, no glow, no gradients, no 3D render.

LAYOUT: follow image 3, a rough layout sketch (not a style reference). There is ONLY ONE ROAD: a single pale sand road that starts at the wooden pier at the bottom, runs all the way around the island along the coast (past regions 1, 5, 10, 15, 20, 30, 40 in that order), then turns inward and spirals up the big mountain in the middle of the island (past 50 at the dark foot of the mountain and 60 on the snowy slope) to end at 70 on the summit. No other roads, no forks, no shortcuts. White dots are minor stages: small pennant flags on short poles right beside the road. Dashed diamonds marked R are empty grass clearings with a few bushes and rocks. Do NOT draw numbers, letters, grid lines or diamond outlines.

REGIONS AND HABITATS: each coloured patch is one region with its own environment, borders blending naturally through terrain. Each numbered diamond is that region's habitat (a creature enclosure on its own platform with a themed border), with the creature of the same number from image 2 standing in it (same design, colours and silhouette as image 2, confident ready stance, about one third of the habitat width):
1 sunny beach next to the pier: round stone sparring ring with a wooden training post (blue droplet creature).
5 marsh: lily-pad pond with mossy rocks and reeds (green frog).
10 rocky cape on the west coast: white-and-red lighthouse on rocks (blue anglerfish).
15 coral shallows in the north-west: giant open clam shells and coral (spiky clam).
20 shipwreck bay on the north coast: half-sunken wooden shipwreck with barrels (red octopus).
30 storm highlands in the north-east: small stone rain-shrine tower under a rain cloud, standing stones (hooded rain wizard).
40 whale bay on the east coast: giant whale-bone arch over a pool (blue whale).
50 abyss caves at the foot of the central mountain: dark purple rock, a sea-cave mouth, glowing blue lantern mushrooms (horned blue serpent).
60 glacier on the upper slope of the mountain: ice cliffs, frozen pool with ice floes (winged blue sea dragon).
70 dragon temple on the summit, the final goal visible from everywhere: ruined temple with broken columns around a round magical whirlpool pool in the crater (young sea dragon), the biggest habitat.

Around the island: open sea with white foam lines along the shore, a sailing boat, seagulls, two or three clouds drifting over the edges.
No text, no UI, no people.
