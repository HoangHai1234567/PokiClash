Concept mockup of ONE SCREEN of a vertical mobile game (9:16 portrait): the player looks at the lower part of a draggable island world map, with the game UI on top.

STYLE: exactly the drawing style of image 1 and image 2: hand-drawn on cream paper, wobbly dark-brown ink outlines, coloured pencil and wax crayon hatching, one flat colour per area, chunky cute doodles. No drop shadows, no cast shadows on the ground, no glossy highlights, no glow, no gradients, no 3D render.

MAP: follow image 4, which is only a rough layout sketch (not a style reference): the landing beach at the bottom, the pale sand road winding upward, the habitat diamonds 1, 5 and 10, part of habitat 15 at the top right, a lagoon at the top middle, sea on the left and bottom right. Do NOT draw numbers, letters, grid lines or diamond outlines. Seen from above at a gentle three-quarter angle.
Each habitat is a small themed diorama on its own diamond-shaped ground patch blending into the grass, with the creature of the same number from image 3 standing on it (same design, colours and silhouette as image 3, about one third of the habitat width):
1: a round stone sparring ring beside tide pools with a wooden training post (blue droplet creature).
5: a lily-pad pond with mossy rocks and reeds (green frog).
10: a white-and-red lighthouse on a rocky outcrop (blue anglerfish).
15 (partly visible at the top right): a reef of giant open clam shells (spiky clam).
Between habitats: grassy plateaus with short hatched brown cliff faces, palm trees, round bushes, ferns, rocks, shells, starfish, a beached rowboat. Water is flat blue with pencil hatching and a white foam line along the shore.

PROGRESS:
- Stages 1 and 5 are cleared: a small triangular cloth flag is planted in each of their habitats. The ground around them and the road up to stage 10 are fully coloured.
- Stage 10 is the current stage: a dashed crayon ring on the ground around its habitat and a small chunky arrow above the anglerfish.
- Past stage 10 (the upper part of the map) the ground is an UNCOLOURED pencil sketch on cream paper (only brown ink lines and light graphite hatching, no colour), but habitat 15 and its creature stay in full colour. The colour boundary is a soft, irregular crayon edge across the road just past stage 10.
- Small round flat stones along the road are minor stages. On the stone between 5 and 10 stands a small blob-like enemy minion.
- COMPANION: on the road just below habitat 10, the small creature from image 5 hops along toward it.

UI (same hand-drawn paper style, flat, no shadows):
- Top: a torn-paper banner with a water-drop emblem and a progress bar with three small chest icons on it.
- Each habitat has a small paper name plate below it with a round level badge. Use short scribble lines instead of real words.
- Bottom: a paper card panel with a small portrait of the next enemy, a big round fight button with a crossed-swords icon, and a small round button with a target icon (jump to current stage).
No readable text, no people.
