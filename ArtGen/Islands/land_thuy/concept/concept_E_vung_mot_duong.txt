Concept art for a mobile creature-collecting game: an adventure world map of ONE large, wide island (much wider than tall), seen from above at a gentle three-quarter angle, shown whole, divided into ten regions, each with its own environment.

KEEP THE LOOK OF IMAGE 4 (an approved concept of this same island): the same rich, detailed environments, the same round stone-bordered creature habitats, the same props, cliffs, rivers, waterfalls and sea, the same density of detail. Only the arrangement of some regions and the road change, following image 3.

STYLE: hand-drawn on cream paper, wobbly dark-brown ink outlines, coloured pencil and wax crayon hatching, one flat colour per area, chunky cute doodles (image 1). No drop shadows, no cast shadows on the ground, no glossy highlights, no glow, no gradients, no 3D render.

LAYOUT: follow image 3, a rough layout sketch (not a style reference). Each coloured patch is one region; borders blend naturally through terrain (cliffs, rivers, tree lines, rock ridges).
THE ROAD: there is ONLY ONE pale sand road, no forks, no side roads, no shortcuts. It starts at the wooden pier at the bottom and visits the habitats strictly in order 1, 5, 10, 15, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60, 70. The road must go RIGHT INTO the heart of every region and arrive at the front of its habitat, then leave it on the other side; it never skirts around a region. It winds naturally like a real country trail: irregular bends, small switchbacks up slopes, a wooden bridge over a stream, stone steps up a cliff, a short boardwalk over water. White dots are minor stages: small red pennant flags on short poles right beside the road. Dashed diamonds marked R are empty grass clearings with a few bushes and rocks. Do NOT draw numbers, letters, grid lines or diamond outlines.

REGIONS AND HABITATS (each habitat is a round raised creature enclosure with a low stone border, decorated for its region; the creature of the same number from image 2 stands inside, same design, colours and silhouette as image 2, confident ready stance, about one third of the habitat width; 50, 60, 70 are bigger):
1 SUNNY BEACH at the bottom, by the pier: golden sand, palm trees, tide pools; stone sparring ring with a wooden training post (blue droplet creature).
5 MARSH in the south-west: reeds, lily-pad ponds, mossy logs; lily-pad pond (green frog).
10 ROCKY CAPE on the west coast: grey rocks, sea cliffs, seagulls; white-and-red lighthouse (blue anglerfish).
15 CORAL SHALLOWS in the north-west corner: turquoise shallow water, pink and orange coral; giant open clam shells with pearls (spiky clam).
20 SHIPWRECK BAY on the north coast: dark cove, broken masts, barrels; half-sunken wooden shipwreck (red octopus).
30 STORM HIGHLANDS in the middle of the island: dark green hills, standing stones, small grey rain clouds; small stone rain-shrine tower (hooded rain wizard).
40 WHALE BAY in the south-east: blue-grey cliffs around a bay, giant whale bones; whale-bone arch over a pool (blue whale).
50 ABYSS CAVES on the east coast: dark navy and purple rocks, crevices, glowing blue lantern mushrooms; dark sea-cave mouth (horned blue serpent).
60 GLACIER in the north-east: snowfield, ice cliffs, frozen pools; frozen lagoon with ice floes (winged blue sea dragon).
70 DRAGON TEMPLE at the top centre, the final region: ancient ruined temple with broken columns beside a big round whirlpool bay (young sea dragon), the biggest habitat.

Around the island: open sea with white foam lines along the shore, a sailing boat, seagulls, two or three clouds drifting over the edges.
No text, no UI, no people.
