A sprite sheet of ONE small wooden sailing ship for the hand-drawn world map in image 1, drawn in EXACTLY the style of image 1: dark brown slightly wobbly ink outlines, coloured pencil and wax crayon texture, bright cheerful flat colours. Flat colouring: each area one even colour, the pencil strokes are only texture; no light and shadow, no shading, no cast shadow, no glossy highlights.

The ship has the same chunky, cute proportions as the little sailboats in image 1 and image 2, but it is a bigger, richer ship: a rounded wooden hull in warm honey brown with darker plank lines, an orange-red stripe along the hull, three round portholes, one mast with a big cream sail carrying a small teal wave emblem, a small teal pennant on top of the mast, a tiny lantern at the stern, a coiled rope and a barrel on the deck.

Draw the SAME ship three times on a pure white background, far apart from each other, not touching, each about the same size (each about a quarter of the picture width), all seen from above at the same high three-quarter angle as the map in image 1:
- LEFT: the ship sailing AWAY from us, toward the top of the picture. We see its stern (the back, with the rudder and the lantern) at the bottom and its bow pointing up. The sail is seen from behind. The deck is visible from above.
- MIDDLE: the ship sailing TOWARD us, toward the bottom of the picture. We see its bow (the front, with a small round figurehead) at the bottom, pointing down at us. The sail is seen from the front with the teal emblem. The deck is visible from above.
- RIGHT: the ship sailing to the RIGHT. Side view from slightly above: bow on the right, stern on the left, the full sail with the emblem.

Each ship floats: its hull ends at the waterline (nothing under the water is drawn), with one thin light turquoise water ring hugging the hull at the waterline. No other water, no waves, no foam spray, no wake, no shadow, no ground. No text, no creatures, no people, no flags other than the pennant.
