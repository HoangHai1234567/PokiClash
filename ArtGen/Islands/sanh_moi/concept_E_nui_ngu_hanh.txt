Concept art, portrait (taller than wide), for the world map of a mobile creature-collecting game. Hand-drawn style exactly like image 1 and image 3: wobbly dark-brown ink outlines, coloured pencil and wax crayon hatching on cream paper, flat colours, chunky cute doodles, bright blue sea with white foam lines. ONE big island shaped like a giant tiered world mountain rising out of the sea, seen from a three-quarter bird's-eye view so every level's cliff face is visible. Five great ledges spiral up around the mountain, each a different element realm, separated by tall cliffs with layered rock bands, with waterfalls pouring from each ledge down to the one below, and long stone stairways and rope bridges climbing between levels. Clouds drift around the upper levels.
