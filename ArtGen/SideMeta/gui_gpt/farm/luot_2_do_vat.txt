STYLE: hand-drawn storybook cartoon for a vertical mobile game: bold dark-brown ink outlines (#2B2118), slightly wobbly like a real pen, coloured-pencil and crayon hatching that stays visible, flat warm colours with light watercolour washes, subtle cream paper grain.
