WHAT IT IS
The background of a game screen: the POTENTIAL CAVE, a deep crystal cave where pets go floor by floor to fight a guardian boss and win magic stones. Mood: adventurous, mysterious but friendly, never scary. Drawn with the technique of image 1 (flat crayon hatching + brown ink line).

The picture is an EMPTY STAGE: game characters and the user interface are added later by the game. The fighting platform must be EMPTY.

LAYOUT (y measured from the top of the picture, 0 % = top edge, 100 % = bottom edge)
1. 0 %-20 %: the cave ceiling. Big dark rounded rocks in deep indigo and slate with a row of chunky stalactites hanging down, a few small teal crystals tucked between them. LOW DETAIL and dark, because a game progress bar is drawn on top of this band.
2. 20 %-50 %: the back wall. In the centre a big rounded cave ARCHWAY leading deeper down: through it we see a winding stone stair going down into a deep teal darkness, with a few far crystal clusters, so it reads as "more floors below". Above the archway, centred at about 24 %-30 %, a BLANK carved stone TABLET fixed on the rock (pale grey-beige stone, rounded corners, about 50 % of the picture width and 6 % of its height, completely empty: the game writes the floor name on it). Left and right of the archway: rock walls with big crystal clusters (cyan-teal crystals on the left, violet crystals on the right), patches of green moss, and two wall torches made of wood with flat orange flames.
3. 50 %-74 %: THE PLATFORM. A big round flat stone fighting platform seen from a slightly raised front view, an ellipse about 85 % of the picture width, its top surface centred at about 60 %. Pale warm sandstone colour, clean and EMPTY, with only one simple carved ring of notches near its rim. Its front edge is a thick rounded rock side with two low steps.
4. 74 %-100 %: foreground, calm and dark because game buttons sit on top of it: big dark rounded boulders and a few dark crystal shards at the left and right edges, a flat dark slate floor in the middle. Keep this band simple and low in detail, mostly one dark colour toward the bottom edge.

COLOURS
Rock: deep indigo, slate blue and dark teal. Crystals: flat cyan-teal and flat violet. Platform: pale warm sandstone beige (the brightest large area in the picture, so the pets standing on it read clearly). Accents: moss green, torch orange. Limited vintage palette, strong contrast between the pale platform and the dark surroundings.

RULES (strict)
- Flat colours only: every area one even colour with crayon hatching as texture. NO lighting: no shadows, no cast shadows, no light rays, no glow or halo around the crystals or torches, no gradients, no shiny highlights, no fog, no sparkles.
- Dark brown ink outlines, slightly wobbly, same weight as image 1.
- NO text, letters, numbers or readable signs anywhere. The stone tablet stays blank.
- No characters, no creatures, no animals anywhere. The platform is empty.
- Do not draw any user interface, frame or logo.
