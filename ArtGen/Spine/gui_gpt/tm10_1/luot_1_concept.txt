TASK: design and draw ONE creature as ONE full picture, in the hand-drawn style of images 1 and 2. It is form 1 of a three-form creature line; its final form is image 3.

THE CREATURE: a BABY SEA DRAGON with ONE head, the FIRST form of the creature line whose later forms are image 3 (three heads) and image 4 (five heads, the final form). Same species, drawn as a chibi hatchling: the head is big, about 40% of the whole creature's height; big round amber eyes (#F0A030) with a dark slit pupil and one small catchlight; a short rounded snout with a small brow ridge pushed down a little so the face reads brave and determined, not sad and not grinning; the mouth slightly open showing two tiny cream fangs and a strip of deep rose (#B8405A) inside.
Colours: scales pale mint-jade (#6ED3BF, LIGHTER than the two later forms) on the back and sides; belly plates pale cream (#F8EBC8) as a row of wide segments along the underside; two tiny golden horn NUBS (#E9B84A, short rounded bumps, no branches); a small tuft of coral-pink (#F28B70) fin mane on the back of the head, three short rounded lobes; a low coral-pink fin ridge of small rounded lobes along the back; two very short cream whisker stubs (#F4EEDC) at the snout. No pearl, no antlers.

THE WATER, exactly in the style of the water in image 3: stylised hand-drawn waves, plump curling crests like the waves of an old woodblock print but simple and chunky: three flat blues (deep #1E68BE, sky #3A96E2, pale #8CD0F6) in broad bands that follow the flow of each wave, and flat white foam (#F4FCFF) as round bumpy scallops on the crests, all with the same brown ink outline.

CANVAS AND FRAMING: landscape 1536x1024, pure-white background. The whole creature and its water are visible, nothing cut by the edges, at least 60 px of white margin all round; the group is about 900 px wide and 800 px tall, centred.

COMPOSITION (important):
- The body is a short plump serpent: it rises out of a small mass of water at the bottom centre in ONE gentle S-curve, the neck clearly visible between the water and the head, and the head at the top facing the viewer's RIGHT (side view, three-quarter at most), as if watching an enemy on the right. No legs, no wings.
- Behind it, to the RIGHT of the body, a small coral-pink fan tail fin of three rounded lobes pokes up out of the water, not touching the body.
- The water: a small ring of two or three little curling waves and round foam around the place where the body leaves the water, a few drops flying up. The front wave overlaps the bottom of the body so no body base is seen. Much less water than in the later forms.

DRAWN FOR A CUT-OUT ANIMATION RIG: the pose must be easy to cut into parts: each head clearly separate from the others and from the body with white or water behind it; necks not crossing each other; mouth slightly open so a strip of the rose inner mouth shows; whiskers and fins not covering the eyes or the mouth; the tail fin apart from the body.

LINE AND COLOUR: the hand-drawn look of the style images: drawn by hand on paper with a dark-brown ink pen (#2B2118), an outline about 6 px thick on this canvas, the same weight on every edge, slightly wobbly like a real pen; every area coloured with coloured pencils and wax crayons, the strokes visible as loose diagonal hatching (strokes thinner than the outline), kept inside the outline. Clean and appealing, neither a crude scribble nor overly detailed.
FLAT COLOUR, NO LIGHT, NO SHADOW: every area is one even colour; the pencil and crayon strokes are texture only, they do not model light. No shading, no darker side, no shadows of any kind (no cast shadow, nothing on the background), no white highlights or shine spots, no gloss, no glow, no rim light, no gradients, no 3D look. The only white allowed is one small catchlight dot in each eye, and the white foam of the water. A darker tone appears only where the design itself has a darker colour.
Plain pure-white background (#FFFFFF): no paper colour or paper grain outside the drawing, no white border or sticker outline around the figure. No text, no labels, no frame, no ground line, no sky, no rocks, no other creature.
