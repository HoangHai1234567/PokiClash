TASK: draw a sheet of NINE SEPARATE PIECES on a pure-white 1536x1024 canvas. They are the loose parts of an animated ball of fire (the burning orb in the head of a mage's staff): a 2D skeleton will stack them (smoke behind, then the flame tongues, then the ball, then its bright heart) and wave them. Each piece is drawn ALONE, complete, not touching any other piece, at the place and size of the layout sheet (image 1), with at least 40 px of white between pieces.

THE PIECES (coordinates are pixels on the 1536x1024 sheet, x from the left, y from the top):
1. THE BALL (about x = 60 to x = 480, y = 90 to y = 510): a perfectly round ball of fire: a ring of red-orange (#E0441F) along the rim and a big orange (#F7902A) inside, the border between the two colours wavy like small flame tongues. A closed ink outline all the way round the circle. No face, no highlight, nothing else on it.
2. THE HEART (about x = 80 to x = 380, y = 580 to y = 880): the bright core that sits on the ball: ONE soft blob of yellow (#FFD45A) with five or six rounded wavy lobes (like a small flower or a splash), a slightly paler yellow (#FFE89A) spot in its middle. NO dark ink outline: its edge is only a thin orange (#F7902A) line.
3. Three nested flat colour zones following its shape: red-orange (#E0441F) outside, orange (#F7902A), and a yellow (#FFD45A) zone inside that starts at the base and ends in a point below the tip. A closed ink outline all the way round, also round the base.

FLAT COLOUR ONLY: the fire looks hot only because its colours are bright: NO glow, NO halo, NO gradients, NO blur, NO highlights, NO shadows, no sparks, no embers, no extra bits.

Plain pure-white background (#FFFFFF), no paper grain outside the pieces, no labels, no numbers, no frame, nothing added.
