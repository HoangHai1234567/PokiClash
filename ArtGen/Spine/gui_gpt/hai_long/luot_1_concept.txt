TASK: design and draw ONE boss monster as ONE full picture, in the hand-drawn style of the style images.

THE CREATURE: a FIVE-HEADED SEA DRAGON, an eastern-style water dragon (long serpent necks, no wings, no legs visible), a boss monster of the water element. Colours, the same on all five heads and necks: scales jade-teal (#2FA79B) on the back and sides; belly plates warm cream-gold (#F2D9A0) as a row of wide segments along the underside of each neck; a low coral-red (#E0563C) fin ridge running along the back of each neck and a coral-red fin mane behind each head; two golden antler-like horns (#E9B84A) per head; two long cream whiskers (#F4EEDC) from the snout; amber eyes (#F0A030) with a dark slit pupil; inside of the mouth deep rose (#B8405A), teeth cream. Each head is a long dragon head seen from the side: long snout, strong lower jaw, brow ridge, determined fierce look, NOT cute and NOT scary-horror. The CENTRE head is the leader: about 30% bigger, with bigger branching antlers and a small cream pearl set on its forehead.

THE WATER, in the style of images 3 and 4: stylised hand-drawn waves, plump curling crests like the waves of an old woodblock print but simple and chunky: three flat blues (deep #1E68BE, sky #3A96E2, pale #8CD0F6) in broad bands that follow the flow of each wave, and flat white foam (#F4FCFF) as round bumpy scallops on the crests, all with the same brown ink outline.

CANVAS AND FRAMING: landscape 1536x1024, pure-white background. The whole monster and its water are visible, nothing cut by the edges, at least 50 px of white margin all round; the group is about 1300 px wide and 880 px tall, centred.

COMPOSITION (important):
- The five necks rise out of ONE churning mass of water at the bottom centre and fan out upward like the ribs of a hand fan. ALL five heads are seen from the side and ALL face the viewer's RIGHT, mouths slightly open, as if watching an enemy on the right.
- The LEADER head is in the centre, the highest, its neck an upright S-curve. Two heads are on the left: one high (neck leaning back to the left, then curving so the head still faces right), one lower. Two heads are on the right: one at middle height reaching forward to the right, one low near the water stretching far to the right. Every neck has its own clear curve; the five heads do NOT overlap each other, and each head is fully visible with white or water behind it.
- The water: a ring of big curling waves around the place where the necks come out, tallest at the two sides, with smaller curls and foam in front; one hump of the dragon's coiled body arches out of the water at the far left and the finned tail tip rises out of the water at the far right. A few drops fly above the waves. The front waves overlap the bottoms of the necks, so no neck base is seen.
- No ground, no rocks, no sky, no other creature.

LINE AND COLOUR: the hand-drawn look of the style images: drawn by hand on paper with a dark-brown ink pen (#2B2118), an outline about 6 px thick on this canvas, the same weight on every edge, slightly wobbly like a real pen; every area coloured with coloured pencils and wax crayons, the strokes visible as loose diagonal hatching (strokes thinner than the outline), kept inside the outline. Clean and appealing, neither a crude scribble nor overly detailed.
FLAT COLOUR, NO LIGHT, NO SHADOW: every area is one even colour; the pencil and crayon strokes are texture only, they do not model light. No shading, no darker side, no shadows of any kind (no cast shadow, nothing on the background), no white highlights or shine spots, no gloss, no glow, no rim light, no gradients, no 3D look. The only white allowed is one small catchlight dot in each eye, and the white foam of the water. A darker tone appears only where the design itself has a darker colour.
Plain pure-white background (#FFFFFF): no paper colour or paper grain outside the drawing, no white border or sticker outline around the figure or its pieces. No text, no labels, no frame, no ground line.
