PIECE RULES (for every piece):
- Draw each piece COMPLETE, including the parts that were hidden behind other pieces in image 1.
- Wherever a piece joins another piece (the JOINT end), continue it about 30 px past the joint with a rounded end, as if it slides under the neighbour piece. Draw the outline around that rounded end too. This hidden overlap keeps the puppet from showing gaps when the joint bends.
- Keep the size, the angle, the bend and the direction each piece has in image 1 (same scale as image 1). Do not rotate, mirror or straighten pieces.
- Leave at least 60 px of pure white between any two pieces; pieces never touch or overlap each other.
- Each piece only once. No shadows under the pieces, no text, no numbers, no labels, no frame, no guide lines.

LINE AND COLOUR: the game's hand-drawn look of the style image, NOT the glossy look of the design image: drawn by hand on paper with a dark-brown ink pen (#2B2118), an outline about 7 px thick on this canvas, the same weight on every edge, slightly wobbly like a real pen; every area coloured with coloured pencils and wax crayons, the strokes visible as loose diagonal hatching (strokes thinner than the outline), kept inside the outline. Clean and appealing, neither a crude scribble nor overly detailed.
FLAT COLOUR, NO LIGHT, NO SHADOW: every area is one even colour; the pencil and crayon strokes are texture only, they do not model light. No shading, no darker side, no shadows of any kind (no cast shadow, no shadow under the figure, on the ground or on the background), no white highlights or shine spots, no gloss, no glow, no rim light, no gradients, no 3D look. The only white allowed is one small catchlight dot in each eye. A darker tone appears only where the design itself has a darker colour.
Plain pure-white background (#FFFFFF): no paper colour or paper grain outside the drawing, no white border or sticker outline around the figure or its pieces (the game adds that itself). No text, no labels, no frame.
