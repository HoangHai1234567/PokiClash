TASK: design and draw ONE creature hero as ONE full-body picture, in a clean animation-ready pose, in the hand-drawn style of the approved hero images.

CREATURE: a small LIVING RAINDROP of the water element, the STARTER creature of a monster-collecting game: instantly recognisable, iconic, appealing, simple readable shapes and few details. A creature, not a human and not a slime puddle: no human face, no clothes except the headband.
FORM 1, the first and smallest form, chibi:
- BODY AND HEAD are ONE plump water-drop shape, wide and round at the bottom, narrowing upward. The top is NOT a straight point: it rises into a tip that CURLS OVER to the viewer's RIGHT like a small breaking wave crest, with a scalloped pale-foam trim along the curl. The curl stays high above the eyes.
- On the upper back (left side of the drop) two or three short wavy deep-blue stripes, like little wave lines.
- On the front of the body a pale-foam BELLY PATCH shaped like an upside-down water drop.
- FACE on the right half of the drop: two big round eyes (cream whites, deep navy irises, one small catchlight dot each), short thick brows slanting down toward the middle, a small mouth slightly OPEN as if shouting a battle cry, showing the deep-rose inside and ONE small fang at the upper lip. DETERMINED, brave, ready to fight; never a happy mascot grin, never scary.
- HEADBAND: a red cloth band tied around the head just above the eyes, the knot at the back of the head (viewer's left) with TWO short ribbon tails fluttering back to the left, clear of the body, not crossing each other.
- ARMS: two short stubby aqua arms ending in small round clenched fists (no fingers drawn), sticking out from the sides of the drop.
- FEET: two short stubby round aqua feet with flat soles.
- TAIL: a small water-splash tail at the lower back on the viewer's left: a short tapering aqua curl with a pale-foam tip that curls upward.

COLOURS (use exactly these, each area one flat colour): body bright aqua (#35BFE6); wave stripes on the back deep ocean blue (#1F5DB5); belly patch and foam trims pale foam aqua (#D6F2FA, never left as white paper); headband and its ribbon tails tomato red (#E2483A) with a thin darker red (#A8302A) knot; eye whites light cream (#FBF6EA); irises deep navy (#1C3A7A) with a black pupil; brows deep ocean blue (#1F5DB5); inside of the mouth deep rose (#B8405A); the fang cream (#FBF6EA).

WATER PARTS (crest curls, foam trims, tail tip, gauntlets) look like the game's water in the water-splash image: plump rounded shapes, round bumpy foam scallops, flat blues, the same brown ink outline. They are SOLID parts of the creature with closed outlines: no loose drops, bubbles, splashes or spray floating around it.

CANVAS AND FRAMING: landscape 1536x1024, pure-white background, NO ground line, no cast shadow. The whole creature is visible, nothing cut by the edges, at least 60 px of white margin all round. It is centred, about 620 px tall from the top of the crest curl to the soles of its feet; its feet are about 140 px above the bottom edge.

POSE (very important: the picture will be cut into separate parts and animated with a 2D skeleton, so parts must be readable and must not overlap):
- Three-quarter side view turned toward the viewer's RIGHT; the face is on the right, the eyes look to the viewer's right.
- Stands on its two stubby feet, both soles flat on one level ground line (no ground drawn), a little boxer's stance: body leaning slightly forward, feet a little apart with white space between them.
- The near arm (on the right) is raised forward and up with its fist at about eye height in front of the body; the far arm (on the left) is raised a little behind, its fist peeking out from behind the back. Each arm is clear of the body outline with a thin white gap where possible; the arms never cross the face.
- The tail sticks out behind on the lower left, the two headband ribbons fly out behind on the upper left; tail, ribbons, arms and the crest curl do not touch each other.

LINE AND COLOUR: the game's hand-drawn look of the approved hero images: drawn by hand on paper with a dark-brown ink pen (#2B2118), an outline about 7 px thick on this canvas, the same weight on every edge, slightly wobbly like a real pen; every area coloured with coloured pencils and wax crayons, the strokes visible as loose diagonal hatching (strokes thinner than the outline), kept inside the outline. Clean and appealing, neither a crude scribble nor overly detailed.
FLAT COLOUR, NO LIGHT, NO SHADOW: every area is one even colour; the pencil and crayon strokes are texture only, they do not model light. No shading, no darker side, no shadows of any kind (no cast shadow, no shadow under the figure, on the ground or on the background), no white highlights or shine spots, no gloss, no glassy or transparent jelly look, no glow, no rim light, no gradients, no 3D look. The only white allowed is one small catchlight dot in each eye. A darker tone appears only where the design itself has a darker colour.
Plain pure-white background (#FFFFFF): no paper colour or paper grain outside the drawing, no white border or sticker outline around the figure (the game adds that itself). No text, no labels, no frame, no props, no effects around the creature.
