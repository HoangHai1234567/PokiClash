- LOTUS BUD: on the middle of the back stands one CLOSED lotus bud, as tall as half the head: a plump teardrop of overlapping pointed petals, pale rose (#F3B5AE) with crimson (#D9443A) tips, sitting on a tiny indigo (#3A4A9C) cup; the bud is NOT open (no petals spread, no seed-pod visible).

- It floats on a SMALL RING OF WATER: a low round puddle of curling water under the belly, about one sixth of the picture's height, with a little curl at each end, a few foam bumps and two drops in the air; the water covers only the bottom edge of the belly.

LINE AND COLOUR: the hand-drawn look of the style images: drawn by hand on paper with a dark-brown ink pen (#2B2118), an outline about 6 px thick on this canvas, the same weight on every edge, slightly wobbly like a real pen; every area coloured with coloured pencils and wax crayons, the strokes visible as loose diagonal hatching (strokes thinner than the outline), kept inside the outline. Clean and appealing, neither a crude scribble nor overly detailed.
FLAT COLOUR, NO LIGHT, NO SHADOW: every area is one even colour; the pencil and crayon strokes are texture only, they do not model light. No shading, no darker side, no shadows of any kind (no cast shadow, nothing on the background), no white highlights or shine spots, no gloss, no glow, no rim light, no gradients, no 3D look. The only white allowed is one small catchlight dot in each eye, the ivory parts of the design, and the white foam of the water. A darker tone appears only where the design itself has a darker colour.
Plain pure-white background (#FFFFFF): no paper colour or paper grain outside the drawing, no white border or sticker outline around the figure. No text, no labels, no frame, no ground line, no sky, no rocks, no other creature.
