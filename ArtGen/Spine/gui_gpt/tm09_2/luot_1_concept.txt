TASK: design and draw ONE fantasy battle creature as ONE full picture, in the hand-drawn style of the style images.

THE CREATURE: a crocodile-like sea reptile of the deep cold ocean, a water-element battle pet. Body plan: long low body carried close to the ground, four short strong legs set slightly apart, a long flat crocodile snout with a strong lower jaw, a long tail. It is a pet-like fantasy animal with a determined fighting face: fierce and ready to strike, appealing, NOT a cheerful mascot and NOT horror. Its horns, back ridge and tail tip are made of OPAQUE pale glacier ice (flat colour, thin ink facet lines), never clear blue gemstone.

FORM 2, the adult "Frost Crocodile King" (final form, a legendary-tier fighter): adult proportions, the head about one quarter of the body length, long and flat like a crocodile's, heavy brow ridges over sharp amber eyes (#F2B544) with a vertical slit pupil. Colours: skin deep petrol blue-green #1F5566 all over the back, head, legs and tail; a row of wide pale belly plates #D9E6EA along the underside from throat to tail tip; chunky rounded armour scales in a slightly lighter petrol #3F7585 on the shoulders, hips and the back of the neck (like a crocodile's bony plates, drawn as a few big rounded bumps with ink lines, not many small scales). TWO BIG HORNS of opaque glacier ice #E6F1F4 (with two or three thin ink facet lines and a few #9DBAC6 edge bands) growing from the back of the skull: thick at the root, sweeping BACKWARD and then curving UP into sharp points like two sickle blades, each about as long as the head. A RIDGE of jagged glacier-ice plates #E6F1F4 along the spine from between the horns to the middle of the tail, tallest over the shoulders, getting smaller toward the tail (like small icebergs standing on its back). The TAIL ends in a heavy MACE of three or four chunky glacier-ice crystals #E6F1F4 clustered together. Mouth: big cream-white teeth #F6FBFD of different sizes standing along both jaws (a few bigger fangs at the front), the inside of the mouth deep blue-green #1B4F66, the tongue #4A9BB5. Claws #2B3F4A, three per foot. A few short curved ink lines on the cheek and throat suggest tough skin folds; keep detail moderate.

POSE FOR A CUT-OUT ANIMATION RIG (important): side view, slightly three-quarter, the creature FACES THE VIEWER'S RIGHT. Standing on all four legs on an invisible flat ground, legs clearly separated from each other and from the belly (white gaps between the legs), no leg crossing another, the tail stretched out to the LEFT and clearly separated from the hind legs, the head clearly separated from the shoulders by a visible neck. The mouth is HALF OPEN (about a quarter of the way) so the inside of the mouth and the tongue are visible between the upper and lower teeth. Nothing overlaps the eye.

CANVAS AND FRAMING: landscape 1536x1024, pure-white background. The whole creature is visible, nothing cut by the edges, at least 60 px of white margin all round; the creature is about 1380 px long from tail mace to snout tip and about 760 px tall from the claws to the horn tips, centred.

LINE AND COLOUR: the hand-drawn look of the style images: drawn by hand on paper with a dark-brown ink pen (#2B2118), an outline about 6 px thick on this canvas, the same weight on every edge, slightly wobbly like a real pen; every area coloured with coloured pencils and wax crayons, the strokes visible as loose diagonal hatching (strokes thinner than the outline), kept inside the outline. Clean and appealing, neither a crude scribble nor overly detailed.
FLAT COLOUR, NO LIGHT, NO SHADOW: every area is one even colour; the pencil and crayon strokes are texture only, they do not model light. No shading, no darker side, no shadows of any kind (no cast shadow, nothing on the ground or background), no white highlights or shine spots, no gloss, no glow, no rim light, no gradients, no 3D look, no transparency effects on the ice (the ice is an opaque flat pale colour with a few thin ink facet lines). The only white allowed is one small catchlight dot in each eye and the teeth. A darker tone appears only where the design itself has a darker colour.
Plain pure-white background (#FFFFFF): no paper colour or paper grain outside the drawing, no white border or sticker outline around the figure. No text, no labels, no frame, no ground line, no water, no ice floor, no mist, no snowflakes, no particles, no other creature.
