TASK: design and draw ONE creature hero as ONE full-body picture, in a clean animation-ready pose, in the hand-drawn style of images 1 to 3.

FORM 1, the young stage: clearly chibi like a monster-collecting game starter: round, plump and compact, big expressive eyes, few details. Cute but already brave.

THE CREATURE: a young SPIKY CLAM, a pet-like battle creature of the water element: a bivalve with a round, plump two-part shell, seen from the side in a three-quarter view. The shell's opening (its mouth) is at the FRONT, facing the viewer's RIGHT; the hinge of the two shells is at the BACK, on the LEFT. The whole shell is round and plump like a ball, about as tall as it is wide (spines not counted).
- TOP SHELL: a fat rounded dome, slate blue (#3D5C9C: a greyish dark BLUE, clearly blue, NOT violet, NOT purple), with three thin concentric growth lines that follow its rim and one cyan band (#5EC2E0) running along the rim; the rim is gently scalloped (wavy). A small round hinge knob at the back-left where the two shells meet.
- BOTTOM SHELL: a deep round bowl of the same slate blue with the same cyan rim band and wavy rim, a little smaller than the top shell.
- SPINES: EXACTLY five short, thick cone spines stand out of the top shell like sea-urchin spines, spread over its upper curve, each pointing straight out from the shell and leaning a little backward; EXACTLY two smaller cone spines stand under the bottom shell pointing down and back. Each spine is ONE flat coral-orange (#F07F55) with only a small pale-peach cap at the very tip (#FFD2B4); a spine is never split lengthwise into a light side and a dark side. Every spine is clearly separate: an ink line where it meets the shell, white space between neighbouring spines.
- SOFT BODY inside the shell: turquoise (#36C2B4) with a few small slate-blue dots. Along the edge of each shell a soft, wavy, frilly turquoise mantle lip shows between the shell rims. The shell is opened at the front like a mouth in a brave battle shout (the gap about 110 px at the front, closing to nothing at the hinge), so we see inside: the inside of the mouth is deep raspberry (#B83A5A), and a small round pearl rests on the lower lip: ONE flat cream colour (#F5F0E6) with one flat lilac patch (#D8CCF0) on its lower part, NO white shine dot, NO highlight.
- FACE: two big round eyes peek out from just under the front rim of the top shell, set in the turquoise mantle above the mouth, side by side at the same height, BOTH pupils looking straight to the right at the enemy (the near eye a little bigger): white eyeballs, a big golden-amber iris (#F2B33D), a dark navy pupil and one small white catchlight dot each. The upper eyelids are flat and slant down toward the front (a confident frown), and the top shell rim sits low over them like a heavy brow, so the face looks brave and determined, a little fighter: cute but NOT goofy, NOT a happy mascot, NOT scary, NOT googly eyes.
- THREE short curly turquoise tentacles, like little whiskers (each a small, fairly thick tapering curl about as long as one eye is wide), stick out of the front corner of the mouth, pointing forward to the right, not crossing each other.
- FOOT: under the front half of the bottom shell a fat, short, rubbery foot of lighter turquoise (#7ED8CB) comes out, shaped like a big thumb bending forward to the right with a rounded tip; the creature stands and hops on it. It is NOT a thin stem and NOT a mushroom stalk. It has NO legs and NO arms.
- Do NOT draw: a tongue hanging out, black empty eye holes, a purple or violet shell, a face drawn on the outside of the shell.

CANVAS AND FRAMING: landscape 1536x1024, pure-white background. The whole creature is visible (all spines and the foot included), nothing cut by the edges, at least 80 px of white margin all round. It is centred, about 660 px wide and about 540 px tall from the tip of the highest spine to the bottom of the foot; its lowest point is about 150 px above the bottom edge.

POSE (very important: the picture will be cut into parts and animated with a 2D skeleton, so the parts must be readable and must not overlap):
- Side three-quarter view turned toward the viewer's RIGHT; the mouth opening and the eyes are at the right.
- The shell is slightly open at the front so the mantle lips, the inside of the mouth and the pearl are clearly seen.
- Nothing covers the eyes. The spines, the whisker tentacles and the foot do not cross each other or the eyes.

LINE AND COLOUR: the hand-drawn look of images 1 to 3: drawn by hand on paper with a dark-brown ink pen (#2B2118), an outline about 7 px thick on this canvas, the same weight on every edge, slightly wobbly like a real pen; every area coloured with coloured pencils and wax crayons, the strokes visible as loose diagonal hatching (strokes thinner than the outline), kept inside the outline. Clean and appealing, neither a crude scribble nor overly detailed.
FLAT COLOUR, NO LIGHT, NO SHADOW: every area is one even colour; the pencil and crayon strokes are texture only, they do not model light. No shading, no darker side, no shadows of any kind (no cast shadow, no shadow under the creature, nothing on the background), no white highlights or shine spots (also none on the pearl and none on the shell), no gloss, no glow, no rim light, no gradients, no 3D look. The only white allowed is one small catchlight dot in each eye. A darker tone appears only where the design itself has a darker colour.
Plain pure-white background (#FFFFFF): no paper colour or paper grain outside the drawing, no white border or sticker outline around the figure. No text, no labels, no frame, no ground line, no water, no bubbles, no effects around the creature.
