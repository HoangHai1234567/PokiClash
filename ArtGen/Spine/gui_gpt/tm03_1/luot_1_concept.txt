TASK: design and draw ONE creature hero as ONE full-body picture, in a clean animation-ready pose, in the hand-drawn style of the reference images. It is a NEW design of our own: do not copy any existing creature or character.

CREATURE: an ANIMAL creature of the water element (no human face, no human body, no human clothes; it wears only the gear listed below, if any), designed like a creature of a monster-collecting game: appealing, simple readable shapes. Its face is DETERMINED and ready to fight: lowered brows, confident focused eyes looking to the viewer's right, mouth slightly open; never a happy mascot grin, never scary.

THE CREATURE: a WHIRLPOOL TADPOLE, a tadpole that is just turning into a little frog. It hatched in the eye of a whirlpool and spins its coiled tail like a propeller to stir up whirlpools.
FORM 1, the young stage: clearly chibi like a monster-collecting game starter, the head-and-body ball is most of its size, big expressive eyes, tiny limbs. Cute but already brave.

DESIGN (follow exactly):
- BODY: ONE big round plump ball (head and body are a single shape, like a fat tadpole), a little wider than tall. The back and the top of the head are cerulean blue (#3FA9E0) with three or four short navy (#24407F) curved stripe marks on the back, each mark curling at its end into a small spiral hook. The belly, the chin and the throat are lemon cream (#F6E28C), one clean area with a smooth edge.
- EYES: two big round bulging frog eyes sitting on TOP of the head, the near one big and the far one peeking out behind it. Golden amber iris (#F2B23A) with a HORIZONTAL dark-brown pupil (a frog's pupil), one small white catchlight dot. A thick cerulean lid drawn low across the top of each eye gives a lowered, determined brow.
- MOUTH: a wide frog mouth along the front of the ball (on the viewer's right), SLIGHTLY OPEN: we can see the flat deep-rose inside of the mouth (#C9506A) and the rounded tip of a pink tongue (#E58A9C) lying in it. No teeth.
- LEGS: two short newly sprouted hind legs under the rear half of the ball, the near one in front and the far one peeking out behind it, each ending in a webbed foot with three round tangerine toe pads (#F28A3C) flat on the ground. Two tiny stubby front legs under the chin, also with tangerine toe pads, held a little forward.
- TAIL (the signature): a long fat tadpole tail growing from the rear of the ball on the viewer's LEFT, with a wide pale-aqua fin (#9FE1F0) running round its whole edge, COILED UP INTO A BIG FLAT SPIRAL of about two and a half turns, like a fern fiddlehead or a snail shell, standing up behind the rear of the body. Navy (#24407F) bands run along the coil so the spiral reads clearly; the core of the tail is cerulean like the back. The coil is about as tall as the body ball. It touches the body only at its root; white space between the coil and the eyes and head.
- Nothing else: no horns, no hair, no fins on the head, no shell, no accessories, no water drops.

CANVAS AND FRAMING: landscape 1536x1024, pure-white background, NO ground line, no cast shadow. The whole creature is visible, nothing cut by the edges, at least 60 px of white margin all round. - Side three-quarter view turned toward the viewer's RIGHT; the mouth and the eyes face the viewer's right.
- It sits low on its hind feet, all feet flat on one level ground line (no ground drawn), leaning slightly forward, a grounded ready-to-fight stance.
- White gaps between the two hind legs, between the front legs and the hind legs, and between each front leg and the belly where possible.
- The coiled tail is on the viewer's LEFT behind the body and never crosses the body, the legs or the eyes.
- Nothing covers the face.

LINE AND COLOUR: the hand-drawn look of the style images: drawn by hand on paper with a dark-brown ink pen (#2B2118), an outline about 7 px thick on this canvas, the same weight on every edge, slightly wobbly like a real pen; every area coloured with coloured pencils and wax crayons, the strokes visible as loose diagonal hatching (strokes thinner than the outline), kept inside the outline. Clean and appealing, neither a crude scribble nor overly detailed.
FLAT COLOUR, NO LIGHT, NO SHADOW: every area is one even colour; the pencil and crayon strokes are texture only, they do not model light. No shading, no darker side, no shadows of any kind (no cast shadow, no shadow under the feet, nothing on the background), no white highlights or shine spots on the skin, no gloss, no wet look, no glow, no rim light, no gradients, no 3D look. The only white allowed is one small catchlight dot in each eye. A darker tone appears only where the design itself has a darker colour.
Plain pure-white background (#FFFFFF): no paper colour or paper grain outside the drawing, no white border or sticker outline around the figure. No text, no labels, no frame, no ground line, no water, no bubbles, no effects around the creature.
