FLAMES AND LAVA: every flame (mane, crest, tufts, tail tip, wings) is a solid part of the creature: a flat shape with a closed dark-brown ink outline, filled with flat crayon colours in at most three clear bands (outer, middle, inner), never left as white paper. CANVAS AND FRAMING: landscape 1536x1024, pure-white background, NO ground line, no cast shadow. The whole creature is visible, nothing cut by the edges, at least 60 px of white margin all round. Plain pure-white background (#FFFFFF): no paper colour or paper grain outside the drawing, no white border or sticker outline around the figure or its pieces (the game adds that itself). No text, no labels, no frame.

Keep the design clean and readable: no text, no background, no extra props, no effects around the creature.
