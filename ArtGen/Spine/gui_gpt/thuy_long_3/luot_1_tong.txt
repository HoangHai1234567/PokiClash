CANVAS AND FRAMING: portrait 1024x1536, pure-white background. The whole character and its weapon are visible, nothing cut by the edges. POSE (very important, the drawing will be cut into pieces for a 2D skeleton animation with bending limbs):
- Three-quarter view turned toward the viewer's RIGHT; the head and eyes look to the viewer's right with a fierce, ready-to-fight expression. A grounded battle-ready stance, not a big action pose.
- Feet planted wide apart, both feet flat on the same ground line, knees slightly bent. The two legs do not overlap: a clear gap between them.
- The arm on the viewer's RIGHT holds the glaive in front of the body at hip height; the glaive is held diagonally, the blade up and forward (to the viewer's right) at about 55 degrees, the butt of the pole down and back near the back foot. The whole glaive is clear of the head and the canvas edges.
- The arm on the viewer's LEFT is held a little away from the body, slightly bent, ending in a clenched fist at hip height, with clear white space between the arm and the body.
- The tail comes out behind the hips on the viewer's LEFT and curves down and back; its tip is clear of the legs and the arm.
- The crest / mane and the horns are fully visible. Nothing covers the face.
- The cape hangs behind the body from both shoulders and flows down behind the back leg and the tail, its outline clear; it does not cover the arms or the front leg.

ANATOMY TO KEEP READABLE (each piece must be a clear shape with its own outline):

- each leg = one continuous leg from the hip to the ankle (thigh and shin, slightly bent at the knee) + a clawed foot with an outline at the ankle;
- the head, the crest / mane, the torso with its armour, the tail, the weapon, the shoulder armour and the hanging cloth are each a clearly outlined shape.

LINE AND COLOUR: the game's hand-drawn look of the style image, NOT the glossy look of the design image: drawn by hand on paper with a dark-brown ink pen (#2B2118), an outline about 7 px thick on this canvas, the same weight on every edge, slightly wobbly like a real pen; every area coloured with coloured pencils and wax crayons, the strokes visible as loose diagonal hatching (strokes thinner than the outline), kept inside the outline. Clean and appealing, neither a crude scribble nor overly detailed.
FLAT COLOUR, NO LIGHT, NO SHADOW: every area is one even colour; the pencil and crayon strokes are texture only, they do not model light. No shading, no darker side, no shadows of any kind (no cast shadow, no shadow under the figure, on the ground or on the background), no white highlights or shine spots, no gloss, no glow, no rim light, no gradients, no 3D look. The only white allowed is one small catchlight dot in each eye. A darker tone appears only where the design itself has a darker colour.
Plain pure-white background (#FFFFFF): no paper colour or paper grain outside the drawing, no white border or sticker outline around the figure or its pieces (the game adds that itself). No text, no labels, no frame.

Keep the design clean and readable: no text, no background, no extra props.
