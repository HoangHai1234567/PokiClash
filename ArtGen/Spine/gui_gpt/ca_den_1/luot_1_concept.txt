TASK: draw ONE creature hero as ONE picture, in a clean animation-ready resting pose, in the hand-drawn style of the approved hero images.

CREATURE: a small, plump LANTERN FISH (a deep-sea anglerfish) that WALKS ON TWO SHORT LEGS and has a LIZARD TAIL, a pet-like battle creature of the water element in a monster-collecting game: iconic, appealing, as simple as possible, readable at a tiny size. Cute but with a grumpy, determined, ready-to-fight face. It is an original design: do not imitate any existing franchise character.
FORM 1, the first and smallest form:
- BODY: head and body are ONE big round shape with NO neck, like a fat round fish that is mostly head and mouth, a little wider than tall (about 1.2 to 1). The UPPER part (forehead, top and back) is deep indigo blue, like a smooth cap covering the top and the back (viewer's left) side. The LOWER FRONT part is one big, round, bulging pale-cream LOWER JAW AND BELLY (jaw, throat and belly are one single round bulge) that takes about half of the front of the body. The cream part is completely plain: no marks, no folds.
- MOUTH: the border between the indigo upper part and the cream lower part at the front IS the mouth: ONE long, very wide, CLOSED mouth line that runs from the far right edge of the face, across the front, to the left cheek, with the corners turned DOWN (a grumpy scowl, not a smile). It is an UNDERBITE: the cream lower jaw sticks out a little further forward than the indigo upper lip.
- TEETH: THREE pointed ivory teeth grow from the edge of the cream lower jaw and point UP, lying over the indigo upper lip: one BIG fang (about 70 px tall, 50 px wide at its base) near the front of the mouth, a little right of the centre of the body; a medium tooth (about 45 px tall) to the left of it; a small tooth (about 30 px tall) near the left corner of the mouth. Simple curved cones with their own ink outline, no gums, no upper teeth.
- EYES: two big round bulging eyes placed high on the head. The NEAR eye is the bigger one, about 120 px across, on the upper middle of the head, a little right of the centre; the FAR eye, about 85 px across, sits at the upper right edge of the head and is partly hidden behind the curve of the head. Each eye: a round pale-cream eyeball with an ink outline, one big round dark pupil (about half the width of the eyeball) looking toward the viewer's RIGHT, one tiny white dot in the pupil, and a heavy indigo upper eyelid that covers the top third of the eyeball and slants down toward the front: a calm, stubborn, battle-ready stare. No eyelashes, no eyebrows.
- LANTERN (the signature and the weapon of this creature): from the top of the forehead, just above and between the eyes, grows ONE long thin flexible STALK, deep indigo like the body, about 16 px thick with its own ink outline. It rises up, bends FORWARD (toward the viewer's right) in one smooth arc like a fishing rod, and ends in a round LANTERN BULB that hangs in the air above and in front of the face. The stalk is about 330 px long along its curve. The bulb is a plump round drop about 100 px across, flat warm yellow, with its own closed ink outline and a small indigo cap where the stalk joins it. The bulb and the upper two thirds of the stalk are surrounded by clear white background: the bulb does NOT touch or overlap the face, the eyes or the teeth (at least 50 px of white between the bulb and the head). The bulb is a plain flat yellow shape: it does NOT glow, no rays, no halo, no sparkles, no light falling on the face.
- FINS: on the back, behind the lantern stalk (upper left of the body), ONE small sail-like back fin: three short indigo spines joined by periwinkle webbing, about 150 px long and 80 px high, leaning backward. On the side of the body, behind the left corner of the mouth, ONE small fan-shaped side fin (about 80 px), periwinkle webbing with indigo ribs, pointing back and slightly down. No other fins.
- LEGS: exactly TWO short, sturdy legs under the body, deep indigo like the body, like the hind legs of a little lizard. The NEAR leg is at the lower-left of the body: a short round thigh that comes out of the body, a short shin, and a flat foot with three stubby round toes pointing right. The FAR leg is at the lower-right, under the cream belly: only its shin and its three-toed foot show below the belly. Each leg is about 90 px tall below the body. No arms, no hands.
- TAIL: a LIZARD tail, not a fish tail: it grows out of the lower back of the body on the viewer's left, thick at its base (about 120 px), and tapers smoothly to a pointed tip, curving up and to the left like a comma. About 300 px long. Deep indigo like the body, with NO fin, NO flame, NO tuft and NO different colour at its end. Clear white background between the tail and the back fin and between the tail and the near leg.
- MARKINGS: three round darker-indigo spots of different sizes (25 to 55 px) on the back, between the back fin and the tail; flat spots with no outline. No scales pattern, no stripes, no gills drawn, no clothes, no accessories.
