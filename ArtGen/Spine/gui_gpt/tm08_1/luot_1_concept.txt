TASK: design and draw ONE monster as ONE full picture, in the hand-drawn style of the style images.

THE CREATURE: the BABY (first form) of the deep-sea serpent dragon in the concept image, a chibi pet-like monster of the water element, round and small but with a brave, determined face (NOT a cheerful mascot, NOT scary).
- PROPORTIONS: chibi: the head is about 45% of the whole height, big round eyes, a short plump body.
- HEAD: big and rounded, seen from the side facing the viewer's RIGHT: a short rounded snout with a tiny hooked upper lip, a small underbite lower jaw with ONE small cream tooth showing, a heavy little brow frowning over one big round amber eye (#F2B544) with a dark round pupil and one small white catchlight. Mouth slightly open, deep plum inside (#5A2A5E). On the back of the head ONE short soft bone-white nub (#F3EBD9) where the adult's spines will grow. NO ears, NO ear fins, NO antlers.
- COLOURS: body slate-indigo (#5058A8), clearly lighter than the adult's deep indigo; belly plates pale moon-mint (#D8F3EA); THREE round flat pale-mint spots (#8FE3D0) on the upper side of the body; fins seafoam mint (#8FE3D0) with thin ink rays.
- DORSAL SAIL: a soft, oversized, floppy mint fin on top of the head and neck like a mohawk, folding over to the back, continuing as a low fin along the short back.
- TAIL: short, ending in a small rounded paddle fin (two little lobes).
- BARBELS: two SHORT chin whiskers, each ending in a small round pale lantern bead (#C9F7EA, a flat circle).
- PECTORAL FINS: two tiny rounded fins behind the head.
- No arms, no legs, no wings, no clothes, no weapon.

POSE AND COMPOSITION (for a cut-out animation rig): side view, the baby rests on its coiled tail like a little spring: the tail makes ONE low loop lying on the ground at the bottom (the paddle fin sticking out to the LEFT, fully visible), the body rises from the loop in a short upright curve, the big head sits on top looking to the viewer's RIGHT, chin up, brave. The barbels hang below the chin against white. Nothing crosses: the tail loop is open (a C-shape, not a closed ring), the fins do not cover the face.
CANVAS AND FRAMING: landscape 1536x1024, pure-white background. The whole creature is visible, at least 80 px of white margin all round; the figure is about 900 px tall and 800 px wide, centred.

LINE AND COLOUR: the hand-drawn look of the style images: drawn by hand on paper with a dark-brown ink pen (#2B2118), an outline about 6 px thick on this canvas, the same weight on every edge, slightly wobbly like a real pen; every area coloured with coloured pencils and wax crayons, the strokes visible as loose diagonal hatching (strokes thinner than the outline), kept inside the outline. Clean and appealing, neither a crude scribble nor overly detailed.
FLAT COLOUR, NO LIGHT, NO SHADOW: every area is one even colour; the pencil and crayon strokes are texture only, they do not model light. No shading, no darker side, no shadows of any kind (no cast shadow, nothing on the background), no white highlights or shine spots, no gloss, no glow, no rim light, no gradients, no 3D look. The only white allowed is one small catchlight dot in each eye. A darker tone appears only where the design itself has a darker colour.
Plain pure-white background (#FFFFFF): no paper colour or paper grain outside the drawing, no white border or sticker outline around the figure. No text, no labels, no frame, no ground line, no water, no rocks, no other creature.
