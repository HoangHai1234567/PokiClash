THE CREATURE: "River Lord Hippo", form 2 of 2 (the FINAL form) of the water-element pet monster of image 5: the same hippo grown into a big, heavy, battle-hardened WAR HIPPO, the lord of the river. It must look clearly STRONGER, FIERCER and MORE IMPRESSIVE than image 5, and a different silhouette, while you can still tell it is the same line.
- PROPORTIONS: NOT chibi at all. Adult hippo proportions: the head is about one third of the figure; a massive barrel body, broad powerful shoulders and four thick pillar legs with big round feet and cream toenails. A LOW, wide, forward-leaning CHARGING stance: the shoulders lower than the rump line, the head LOWERED and thrust forward toward the right at shoulder height (not raised up), front legs braced wide apart with the near front leg stepping forward.
- HEAD: the same broad hippo muzzle and nostril bumps as image 5 but heavier, a strong square lower jaw; the mouth OPEN about 40 degrees in a battle roar showing the rose inside, the tongue and the teeth; two LONG curved ivory TUSKS (#F3E6C8) curving up from the lower jaw, each with a bronze ring near its base. Small fin-ears, now dark teal with a notch.
- ARMOUR (bronze, moderate, not a full knight suit): a bronze war-helm plate over the forehead and the top of the muzzle (#C28A3E) with a wave-crest shaped ridge on top and small teal patina inlays (#4FB3A0); a bronze collar around the neck holding one round cream pearl at the front; a curved bronze plate on the near shoulder.
- BACK FINS: a row of FOUR tall SAIL FINS along the back, each shaped like a curling WAVE CREST leaning forward: deep teal (#1F8A8A) with a few dark spines (#165F63) and a scalloped white foam cap (#F4FCFF) on the curled tip, the front ones the tallest, sharper and more dramatic than the rounded fins of image 5.

LINE AND COLOUR: the hand-drawn look of the style images: drawn by hand on paper with a dark-brown ink pen (#2B2118), an outline about 6 px thick on this canvas, the same weight on every edge, slightly wobbly like a real pen; every area coloured with coloured pencils and wax crayons, the strokes visible as loose diagonal hatching (strokes thinner than the outline), kept inside the outline. FLAT COLOUR, NO LIGHT, NO SHADOW: every area is one even colour; the pencil and crayon strokes are texture only, they do not model light. Plain pure-white background (#FFFFFF): no paper colour or paper grain outside the drawing, no white border or sticker outline around the figure. No text, no labels, no frame, no ground line, no puddle, no splash under the feet.
