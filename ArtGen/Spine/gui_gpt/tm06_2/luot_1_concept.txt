CREATURE: It is an ANIMAL creature of a monster-collecting game (not a human: no human face, no human body, no clothes; only the carried object and its harness listed below), simple readable shapes. Its face is DETERMINED and ready to fight: lowered brows, focused eyes looking to the viewer's right; never a happy mascot grin, never scary.
Its species must read at once as a SALAMANDER (an axolotl-like newt): a long thick tail, a wide flat head with feathery external gills, smooth skin. It is NOT a frog, NOT a toad, NOT a tadpole: no frog legs folded under a round body, no bulging frog eyes, no beak, no shell, no plate or leaves on the head.

THE CREATURE: a GIANT MOUNTAIN STORM SALAMANDER, the grown-up form of the rain salamander of image 1, a mythical water amphibian that carries a whole thunderstorm sealed in a bronze urn on its back like a shell. This line belongs to one of the island's bosses: make it especially beautiful and refined (elegant shapes, rich but readable ornament).
- Body: like a real giant salamander: a long, broad, flat, heavy body carried low just above the ground, a few wavy ink lines for the loose skin folds along the flank; a white gap between the belly and the ground line.
- Head: very broad and flat, rounded at the front like a shovel (a round blunt snout, NOT a long crocodile or lizard snout), wider than tall, held low and forward; small sharp narrowed eyes set high near the front, amber irises (#F0A030) with a dark slit pupil and one small catchlight dot, a heavy brow; the very wide mouth runs far back along the side of the head and is slightly open, showing the deep-rose inside (#B8405A), a row of small pointed cream teeth and two small fangs.
- Gills: on each side of the head behind the jaw, THREE BIG feathery gill fronds like long fern branches, crimson red (#C8383A), much bigger and longer than those of form 1, sweeping up and back like a war crest; the far-side fronds only peek out above the head.
- Skin: storm-cloud grey-violet (#5A5878) on the head, back, legs and tail, with a darker grey-violet band (#3E3C58) along the top of the back and the top of the tail; white-foam markings (#EAF2F8) shaped like small curling wave crests on the back, the flanks, the legs and the tail. The throat and the belly are a row of wide amber-orange belly plates (#E8782E) separated by thin ink lines, visible along the underside.
- Legs: four short, thick, bowed legs splayed a little sideways like a salamander's, broad webbed feet with four toes and short dark claws (#2B2118).
- Back fin and tail: a low wavy fin crest, pale storm-blue membrane (#8FB3E0) with thin dark-brown ink spines, runs from the back of the head along the spine (hidden behind the urn) and continues as a taller fin along the top and the bottom of the long, thick, flattened paddle tail.
- What it carries: a big BRONZE WATER URN lying on its back like a shell, as wide as about half of the body: old verdigris bronze (#4E9E8A) with gold-bronze bands (#D4A23F) and a raised curling-wave relief around its belly, a heavy domed bronze lid with a ring handle, the short neck of the urn pointing up and a little forward. It is tied on with a thick indigo rope harness (#2A3F78) with knots: one rope loops around the chest just behind the front legs, one around the belly just before the hind legs, and a bronze collar band (verdigris with a gold rim) sits around the neck.

LINE AND COLOUR: the hand-drawn look of the style images: drawn by hand on paper with a dark-brown ink pen (#2B2118), an outline about 7 px thick on this canvas, the same weight on every edge, slightly wobbly like a real pen; every area coloured with coloured pencils and wax crayons, the strokes visible as loose diagonal hatching (strokes thinner than the outline), kept inside the outline. Clean and appealing, neither a crude scribble nor overly detailed.
FLAT COLOUR, NO LIGHT, NO SHADOW: every area is one even colour; the pencil and crayon strokes are texture only, they do not model light. The only white allowed is one small catchlight dot in each eye (cream spots and foam stripes are flat skin markings, not highlights). A darker tone appears only where the design itself has a darker colour.
Plain pure-white background (#FFFFFF): no paper colour or paper grain outside the drawing, no white border or sticker outline around the figure. No text, no labels, no frame, no ground line, no water, no rain, no effects around it.
