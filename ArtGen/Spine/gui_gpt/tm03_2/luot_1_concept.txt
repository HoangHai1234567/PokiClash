TASK: design and draw ONE creature hero as ONE full-body picture, in a clean animation-ready pose, in the hand-drawn style of the reference images. It is a NEW design of our own: do not copy any existing creature or character.

CREATURE: an ANIMAL creature of the water element (no human face, no human body, no human clothes; it wears only the gear listed below, if any), designed like a creature of a monster-collecting game: appealing, simple readable shapes. Its face is DETERMINED and ready to fight: lowered brows, confident focused eyes looking to the viewer's right, mouth slightly open; never a happy mascot grin, never scary.

THE CREATURE: the WHIRLPOOL HORNED FROG, the grown-up form of the whirlpool tadpole of image 2. Its tail is gone; the whirlpool power now lives in its swept-back brow horns and its huge throat sac, and its croak sends spinning rings of water. It wears the lily pad of the old pond king as a mantle.
FORM 2, the FINAL stage of this two-stage line: fully grown and NOT chibi at all: a big, heavy, broad horned frog with a powerful athletic body, the head about one third of its body length, sharp narrowed eyes. The coolest and most beautiful design of the line: fierce, proud and battle-hardened, but NOT scary or monstrous. A sturdy, wide, heavy frog (not a slim ninja frog, not a human-like frog warrior).

DESIGN (follow exactly):
- COLOURS (deeper and bolder than form 1, like a poison-dart frog): back, head top and the outside of the legs vivid deep cobalt blue (#2457C5). PATTERN ON THE BODY AND HEAD: round black-navy (#1A1F3D) spots of different sizes, and on the back and the thighs a few RIPPLE MARKS: a black-navy dot with one or two thin concentric rings around it, like a raindrop falling on a pond. ABSOLUTELY NO SPIRALS, NO SWIRLS and NO CURLS anywhere on the body, the head or the belly. Only on the outside of the four lower legs: two or three short black-navy curved hook stripes, like the stripe marks of form 1. The belly, the chin and the inside of the legs are saffron lemon (#F5CE3A), plain.
- HEAD: a wide flat frog head with a broad mouth. Two big golden amber eyes (#F09A2A) with HORIZONTAL dark pupils set high on the head, narrowed under heavy lowered brows, one small white catchlight dot each. BROW HORNS: from the top of each upper eyelid grows one pointed flat horn, like the eyelid horns of a real horned frog: a short leaf-shaped skin spike, cobalt with a pale aqua (#7FD3E0) back edge, swept straight BACKWARD and a little upward as if blown back by a whirlwind, its tip pointing to the viewer's LEFT. The two horns are straight or gently curved, never curled, never rolled into a spiral, never ram or goat horns, no rings on them.
- MOUTH: the wide mouth SLIGHTLY OPEN in a fierce snarl: the mouth corners turn DOWN (never a smile, never a smirk); we see the flat deep-rose inside of the mouth (#B8405A), a pink tongue lying in it, and a row of small pointed cream teeth along the jaws with two slightly bigger fangs.
- THROAT SAC: under the chin a round throat sac about as wide as the head, half inflated like a ball, plain saffron lemon with NO spiral on it, only two or three short thin black-navy ripple arcs along its lower edge. It hangs below the chin, in front of the chest, and does NOT cover the front legs.
- MANTLE AND GEAR: a big round LILY PAD worn as a short mantle over the shoulders and the top of the back: flat TEAL-GREEN (#2F8A7A, a blue-green water-plant colour, not grass green) with lighter teal (#5FB8A6) vein lines radiating from its centre and the typical notch cut into its edge, its rim slightly wavy. It is tied across the chest with a twisted reed rope (straw #D8B867) fastened by one round gold clasp (#E2A93B) engraved with a small spiral (the only spiral of the design). The mantle stays on the shoulders and back: it does not hang over the legs or the face. Each front forearm wears a band of the same twisted reed rope. No shells, no metal armour plates.
- LIMBS: thick strong front legs with webbed hands of three long fingers, each finger ending in a round tangerine pad (#EE7F38) with a short dark claw. Huge folded hind legs (big frog thighs) with long webbed feet, tangerine toe pads.
- No tail, no hair, no cloth cape (the lily pad is the only mantle), no weapon, no water or effects.

CANVAS AND FRAMING: landscape 1536x1024, pure-white background, NO ground line, no cast shadow. The whole creature is visible, nothing cut by the edges, at least 60 px of white margin all round. It is centred, about 660 px tall from the top of the horns to the feet, and about 1150 px wide, with at least 100 px of white margin on every side; its lowest point is about 120 px above the bottom edge.

POSE (very important: the picture will be cut into parts and animated with a 2D skeleton, so body parts must be readable and must not overlap):
- Side three-quarter view turned toward the viewer's RIGHT; the head is at the right, the eyes look to the viewer's right.
- A low crouched READY-TO-POUNCE stance on all four limbs, all hands and feet flat on one level ground line (no ground drawn): the two front legs straight and planted wide apart under the chest, the near front leg in front, the far front leg peeking out behind it with a white gap between them; the big hind legs folded at the rear on the viewer's left, the near hind leg in front, the far hind foot visible behind it with a white gap. The head is raised forward, the mouth toward the viewer's right.
- White gaps between the front legs and the hind legs, and between the front legs and the throat sac.
- Nothing covers the face; the swept-back brow horns, the lily-pad mantle and the throat sac do not cross each other or the eyes.

LINE AND COLOUR: the hand-drawn look of the style images: drawn by hand on paper with a dark-brown ink pen (#2B2118), an outline about 7 px thick on this canvas, the same weight on every edge, slightly wobbly like a real pen; every area coloured with coloured pencils and wax crayons, the strokes visible as loose diagonal hatching (strokes thinner than the outline), kept inside the outline. Clean and appealing, neither a crude scribble nor overly detailed.
FLAT COLOUR, NO LIGHT, NO SHADOW: every area is one even colour; the pencil and crayon strokes are texture only, they do not model light. No shading, no darker side, no shadows of any kind (no cast shadow, no shadow under the feet, nothing on the background), no white highlights or shine spots on the skin, no gloss, no wet look, no glow, no rim light, no gradients, no 3D look. The only white allowed is one small catchlight dot in each eye. A darker tone appears only where the design itself has a darker colour.
Plain pure-white background (#FFFFFF): no paper colour or paper grain outside the drawing, no white border or sticker outline around the figure. No text, no labels, no frame, no ground line, no water, no bubbles, no effects around the creature.
