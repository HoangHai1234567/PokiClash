TASK: design and draw ONE creature as ONE full picture, in the hand-drawn style of the style images. Every design detail below is required; the reference creatures give only the drawing style and attitude, never their design.

THE CREATURE: "Little River Hippo", form 1 of 2 of a water-element pet monster: a young, plump, CHIBI WATER HIPPO. It looks determined and ready to fight (a little warrior), cute but not a smiling mascot and not scary.
- BODY: CHIBI and ROUND: a compact ball almost as tall as it is long; the head is BIG, about half of the whole figure and as big as the body, with a short round body behind it; NOT a long horizontal body like a turtle, a pig or a dinosaur. EXACTLY FOUR short, thick, stubby legs and four feet in total: the near front and near hind legs in front (bigger), the far front and far hind legs behind them (smaller, each visible just to the left of its near leg), never a fifth foot; the legs are clearly visible below the belly (about one sixth of the figure height) and separated from each other; each foot is round with three small cream toenails.
- HEAD: a wide, rounded hippo MUZZLE in front, shaped like a big bean, with two small round nostril bumps on top of its tip. The MOUTH is open about 30 degrees: we see the rose inside of the mouth and a short pink tongue lying on the lower jaw; two short stubby cream TUSKS stick UP from the front of the lower jaw, and a few small rounded cream teeth sit along the jaws. Two big round EYES sit HIGH on top of the head like little domes (hippo style): golden-yellow irises (#F2C230), a big dark pupil, one small white catchlight dot, and a thick determined frowning brow above each eye. Two small EARS shaped like little rounded fins stand on top of the head behind the eyes.
- BACK FINS: a crest of THREE rounded SAIL FINS runs along the middle of the back from behind the ears to the hips, the middle one the tallest (about one fifth of the figure's height); each is a soft webbed fin with three or four dark spines and a scalloped wavy edge, leaning back. Rounded and soft, NOT pointed blades or leaves.
- TAIL: a short, thick, tapering tail stub at the rear, visible between the rump and the water; from its tip rises a living WATER SPIRIT: one plump curling wave crest of water, about half as tall as the head, curling up and forward over itself with a round foam scallop on the curl, attached to the tail tip.
- COLOURS (keep exactly): body SLATE BLUE-GREY (#7A93BE, a greyish blue like a wet river stone, NOT lavender, NOT purple) on the head, back, sides and legs; muzzle, chin, throat and belly PALE PEACH (#F6D5C0); a few round pale-blue spots like bubbles (#BFD0EA) scattered on the back and the top of the head; sail fins and fin-ears SEAFOAM MINT (#86DCC4) with dark teal spines (#2D8C86); inside of the mouth rose (#C9506A), tongue pink (#EE8C9C); tusks, teeth and toenails cream (#F8EBD0); the water spirit as described below.
- NOT a fish, NOT a frog, NOT a whale, NOT a pig: clearly a little hippo with fins.

THE WATER SPIRIT, in the look of the game's water of image 4: plump rounded water shapes, broad flat bands of three blues following the flow (deep #2B7FD6, sky #4FB6EE, pale #A6DEF8), round bumpy white foam scallops (#F4FCFF) on the curl, brown ink outline. It is WATER, not fire: no flame tongues, no flickering points, no glow.

CANVAS AND FRAMING: landscape 1536x1024, pure-white background. The whole creature is visible, nothing cut by the edges, at least 90 px of white margin all round; the figure is about 860 px wide and 760 px tall including the fins and the water spirit, centred.
VIEW AND POSE: seen from the side in a slight three-quarter view, the creature faces the viewer's RIGHT. It stands on all four legs in a sturdy, ready-to-fight stance, the head a little lowered and pushed forward toward the right as if about to bite.

POSE FOR A 2D BONE RIG (important): all four legs are clearly separated from each other and from the belly, none crossing another; the far legs show a little behind and between the near legs, not hidden; the tail and its water spirit stand free from the body with white between them and the back; the mouth is open enough to see inside it.

LINE AND COLOUR: the hand-drawn look of the style images: drawn by hand on paper with a dark-brown ink pen (#2B2118), an outline about 6 px thick on this canvas, the same weight on every edge, slightly wobbly like a real pen; every area coloured with coloured pencils and wax crayons, the strokes visible as loose diagonal hatching (strokes thinner than the outline), kept inside the outline. Clean and appealing, neither a crude scribble nor overly detailed.
FLAT COLOUR, NO LIGHT, NO SHADOW: every area is one even colour; the pencil and crayon strokes are texture only, they do not model light. No shading, no darker side, no shadows of any kind (no cast shadow, no shadow under the feet, nothing on the background), no white highlights or shine spots, no gloss, no glow, no rim light, no gradients, no 3D look. The only white allowed is one small catchlight dot in each eye and the white foam of the water. A darker tone appears only where the design itself has a darker colour.
Plain pure-white background (#FFFFFF): no paper colour or paper grain outside the drawing, no white border or sticker outline around the figure. No text, no labels, no frame, no ground line, no puddle, no splash under the feet.
