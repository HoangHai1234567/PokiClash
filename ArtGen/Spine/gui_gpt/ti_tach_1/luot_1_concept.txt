CREATURE: a small LIVING BLOB OF WATER, the STARTER creature of the water element in a monster-collecting game: iconic, appealing, as simple as possible, readable at a tiny size.
FORM 1, the first and smallest form:
- BODY: ONE plump DOME of water, like a big drop of water resting on a table. - EYES (the signature of this creature): two TALL UPRIGHT OVALS, each filled with flat very pale ice blue and surrounded by a THICK deep-ocean-blue ring (about 10 px).
