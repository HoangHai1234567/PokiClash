THE CREATURE (the FIRST form of its line, a common fighter of the water element): a young HERMIT CRAB that lives inside a heavy SEA BOULDER and carries it on its back like a house. Chibi proportions: short chunky limbs, the head plate and the eyes big for its body. Personality: the tough, stubborn little guard of the tide pools; determined frowning face, ready to fight. NOT a smiling mascot, NOT scary.

BODY PARTS AND COLOURS:
- BOULDER HOUSE (on its back, the left part of the figure): one big, rounded, lumpy sea boulder, a little bigger than the crab's own front body, with a wide round opening on its RIGHT side out of which the crab's front body comes. Colour: dark wet basalt, flat charcoal blue-grey (#4B5160), with a few thin lighter grey crack lines (#7A8290). On it: a cluster of five small cream barnacles (#F4EEDC; little cones with a dark slit on top), two or three flat coral-pink crust patches (#EE8C8C) shaped like lichen, and ONE small tuft of three green seaweed ribbons (#5DB34F) growing from the top and flopping back to the left. The crab's body fills the opening; only a thin dark crescent of the inside of the opening (#2E3240) shows around it.
- HEAD AND FRONT BODY: a rounded shield-shaped head plate in flat slate blue (#6A8BBB) with two small blunt points above the eyes; its lower part and the face around the mouth are pale sea-foam aqua (#BFE6E2).
- EYES: two SHORT, THICK eye stalks (slate blue) rising from the top of the head plate, side by side; each eye has an amber-gold iris (#F2B33D), a large dark pupil and ONE small white catchlight dot; a slate-blue armoured eyelid cap over the top of each eye gives a stern, frowning look.
- ANTENNAE: two long thin coral-pink antennae (#E98A86) from between the eye stalks, sweeping up and back over the boulder in gentle curves, apart from each other.
- MOUTH: a small mouth under the eyes, between two little pale-aqua mouth flaps, slightly open so the deep rose inside (#C2505E) shows.
- BIG CLAW (on the near side, the viewer's side): a chunky HAMMER CLAW: the palm is a big rounded block about as big as the head plate, slate blue with a pale-aqua inner side and a few flat darker slate-blue round bumps (#56749F); two short, thick fingers with dark navy tips (#2E3B57), slightly open. It sits on a short, thick, two-segment arm.
- SMALL CLAW (on the far side): a slim little pincer on a thin two-segment arm, slate blue with dark navy finger tips, open.
- LEGS: four short walking legs (two on the near side in front, two on the far side behind them), each with three slate-blue segments, pale-aqua joint bands and a pointed dark navy tip.

POSE (important: the picture will be cut into parts for a 2D bone animation rig):
- 3/4 side view facing the viewer's RIGHT: the boulder on the left half, the crab's face and claws on the right half.
- The crab stands on its four walking legs, spread apart: no leg crosses another leg, every leg tip clearly separate. The boulder rests on the crab's back; its bottom stays above the leg tips (it does not touch the ground).
- The big claw is held forward and low on the right, in front of the chest, slightly open; it does NOT cover the face or the legs. The small claw is raised up beside the eye stalks, open. Leave white gaps between the claws, the legs and the body wherever possible.
- The mouth is slightly open so the inside of the mouth shows. Both eyes visible.

CANVAS AND FRAMING: landscape 1536x1024, pure-white background. The whole creature is visible, nothing cut by the edges; it is about 1000 px wide and 760 px tall, centred, with at least 70 px of white margin all round.

LINE AND COLOUR: the hand-drawn look of the style images: drawn by hand on paper with a dark-brown ink pen (#2B2118), an outline about 7 px thick on this canvas, the same weight on every edge, slightly wobbly like a real pen; every area coloured with coloured pencils and wax crayons, the strokes visible as loose diagonal hatching (strokes thinner than the outline), kept inside the outline. Clean and appealing, neither a crude scribble nor overly detailed.
FLAT COLOUR, NO LIGHT, NO SHADOW: every area is one even colour; the pencil and crayon strokes are texture only, they do not model light. No shading, no darker side, no shadows of any kind, no white highlights or shine spots, no gloss, no glow, no rim light, no gradients, no 3D look. The only white allowed is one small catchlight dot in each eye (and the cream colours named in the design). A darker tone appears only where the design itself has a darker colour.
NO SHADOWS: no drop shadow, no hard offset shadow, no cast shadow, no shadow under the feet or on the ground or background.
Plain pure-white background (#FFFFFF): no paper colour or paper grain outside the drawing, no white border or sticker outline around the figure. No text, no labels, no frame, no ground line, no water, no splashes, no bubbles, no other creature, no other rocks than the creature's own house.
