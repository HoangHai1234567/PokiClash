- Stands on its FOUR legs, all paws flat on one level ground line (no ground drawn), a grounded ready-to-fight stance: the front a little lower, head forward.

LINE AND COLOUR: the game's hand-drawn look of the style images: drawn by hand on paper with a dark-brown ink pen (#2B2118), an outline about 7 px thick on this canvas, the same weight on every edge, slightly wobbly like a real pen; every area coloured with coloured pencils and wax crayons, the strokes visible as loose diagonal hatching (strokes thinner than the outline), kept inside the outline. Plain pure-white background (#FFFFFF): no paper colour or paper grain outside the drawing, no white border or sticker outline around the figure or its pieces (the game adds that itself). No text, no labels, no frame.
