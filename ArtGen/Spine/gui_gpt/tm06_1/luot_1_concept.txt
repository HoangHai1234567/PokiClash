THE CREATURE: a chibi RAIN SALAMANDER pup, a young mythical water amphibian of the water element that carries the first rain of the season in a gourd on its back.
- Head: wide, flat and rounded, like a broad bean seen from the side, longer than tall. - Tail: long and thick, with a soft low fin (pale blue #9CC0E8) along its top and bottom edges, the tip curling up.
