TASK: design and draw ONE creature as ONE full picture, in the hand-drawn style of the style images.

THE CREATURE: an adult SEA-UNICORN WHALE, a narwhal-like whale of the water element that carries a big blooming LOTUS on its back: a sacred guardian beast, majestic and battle-ready, drawn as a stylised game monster (plump streamlined whale body, big expressive head, simple shapes), NOT realistic, NOT cute-chibi, NOT scary-horror. It is seen from the SIDE and faces the viewer's RIGHT.
- BODY: a long, muscular whale body, deep indigo-blue (#3A4A9C) on the back and sides, a wide ivory (#F4ECD8) belly and throat with four or five simple ink grooves along the throat (whale pleats). The dividing line between indigo and ivory is one clean wavy ink line; NO swirl patterns, NO spots, NO scales. Three thin GOLD (#E8B64A) ring bands around the body at the root of the tail, like armour rings.
- HEAD: a big rounded whale head with a strong brow ridge; the mouth is a long line slightly OPEN, showing a strip of deep wine-red (#8B2F45) inside the mouth and a row of small pointed ivory teeth on the upper and lower jaw (the lower jaw must be clearly its own shape below the mouth line); one eye on the visible side: a narrow, sharp, determined eye with a round cyan iris (#45CFE0), a dark pupil and one small white catchlight; a flat GOLD brow crest shaped like a single lotus petal lies on the forehead at the root of the tusk.
- TUSK: ONE long straight spiral tusk like a narwhal's, GOLD (#E8B64A) with a thin ink spiral groove winding along it, growing from the top of the snout and pointing forward and slightly upward to the right, about as long as the head is wide; it must stand alone against white with nothing crossing it.
- LOTUS: one large fully OPEN lotus flower standing on the middle of the back, as tall as the head: two rings of plump pointed petals, the outer petals crimson (#D9443A), the inner petals a lighter rose (#EE8A80), and a round GOLD seed-pod in the centre with a few dark ink dots; the flower is fully visible against white, it does not overlap the head or the tail.
- FINS: two broad paddle-shaped pectoral flippers, indigo with a crimson (#D9443A) edge band: the NEAR flipper points down-forward away from the body (clearly separated from the belly by white), the FAR flipper peeks out behind the body above the back, in front of the lotus; one tall dorsal fin behind the lotus, indigo with a crimson edge, swept back; the TAIL ends in a wide two-lobed fluke seen flat (like a fan), indigo on top with crimson tips and an ivory underside stripe.
- The whole creature looks powerful and noble: chest raised, head up, eye fixed on an enemy to the right.

THE WATER, in the style of image 2: stylised hand-drawn waves, plump curling crests, simple and chunky: three flat blues (deep #1E68BE, sky #3A96E2, pale #8CD0F6) in broad bands that follow the flow, and flat white foam (#F4FCFF) as round bumpy scallops on the crests, all with the same brown ink outline.

CANVAS AND FRAMING: landscape 1536x1024, pure-white background. The whole creature and its water are visible, nothing cut by the edges, at least 60 px of white margin all round; the group is about 1300 px wide and 820 px tall, centred.
POSE (important, the drawing will be cut into parts for a 2D cut-out animation): side view, head at the RIGHT, tail at the LEFT. The body makes one gentle S: the chest and head rise toward the upper right (head tilted up about 20 degrees), the back arches, the tail curves down and then its fluke sweeps up at the far left so the whole fluke is visible against white. The near flipper, the far flipper, the dorsal fin, the lotus, the tusk and the fluke each stand clear with white around them; no part crosses another part. The mouth is slightly open so the inside of the mouth and the teeth are visible.
- It rides on a LOW CUSHION OF WATER: a long low swell of curling water under the belly, about one fifth of the picture's height, with two small curls and foam at its two ends and a few drops in the air; the water covers only the bottom edge of the belly and the base of the near flipper, nothing else.

LINE AND COLOUR: the hand-drawn look of the style images: drawn by hand on paper with a dark-brown ink pen (#2B2118), an outline about 6 px thick on this canvas, the same weight on every edge, slightly wobbly like a real pen; every area coloured with coloured pencils and wax crayons, the strokes visible as loose diagonal hatching (strokes thinner than the outline), kept inside the outline. Clean and appealing, neither a crude scribble nor overly detailed.
FLAT COLOUR, NO LIGHT, NO SHADOW: every area is one even colour; the pencil and crayon strokes are texture only, they do not model light. No shading, no darker side, no shadows of any kind (no cast shadow, nothing on the background), no white highlights or shine spots, no gloss, no glow, no rim light, no gradients, no 3D look. The only white allowed is one small catchlight dot in each eye, the ivory parts of the design, and the white foam of the water. A darker tone appears only where the design itself has a darker colour.
Plain pure-white background (#FFFFFF): no paper colour or paper grain outside the drawing, no white border or sticker outline around the figure. No text, no labels, no frame, no ground line, no sky, no rocks, no other creature.
