- top-left cell: the HEAD, UPPER PART ONLY, side view facing the viewer's RIGHT: the big rounded indigo (#3A4A9C) whale skull with its strong brow ridge, the one narrow fierce eye (round cyan #45CFE0 iris, dark pupil, one small white catchlight), the flat gold (#E8B64A) lotus-petal brow crest on the forehead, and the upper jaw with its row of small pointed ivory teeth along the bottom edge; a strip of ivory (#F4ECD8) along the lower edge where the throat begins. NO tusk, NO lower jaw, NO neck. The back end of the head (where the body attaches, on the left) is a smooth rounded stump. About 440 px wide.
- top-middle cell: the LOWER JAW alone, side view facing right: one long wedge, ivory (#F4ECD8) with three ink throat grooves along its underside, the lower teeth (small pointed ivory) standing along its top edge, a strip of the wine-red (#8B2F45) inner mouth visible above the gum line; the back end (the jaw hinge, on the left) is a rounded knob. About 340 px wide, 120 px tall.
- top-right cell: a MOUTH INTERIOR patch: one simple soft rounded blob of flat wine-red (#8B2F45) with a slightly darker throat spot (#6A1F33) in the middle, brown ink outline, about 240 px wide and 170 px tall. No teeth.
- bottom-left cell: the TUSK alone, drawn perfectly STRAIGHT and HORIZONTAL like a ruler: the thick rounded root at the LEFT (with one gold ring band around it), tapering to a sharp point at the RIGHT; flat gold (#E8B64A) with one thin ink spiral groove winding along its whole length. About 470 px long and 72 px thick at the root.
- bottom-middle cell: ONE CLOSED EYELID for the head above: a leaf-shaped patch of the head's indigo skin (#3A4A9C, crayon hatching) with the brown ink outline all round and ONE thick dark-brown curved line across its lower half (the shut eye, curving down toward the snout like a stern frown). About 150 px wide and 95 px tall.
- bottom-right cell: the DORSAL FIN alone: a tall fin swept back to the LEFT (like a shark fin leaning back), indigo with a crimson (#D9443A) edge band along its back edge, the root at the bottom cut flat and slightly widened. About 300 px wide and 330 px tall.

LINE AND COLOUR: the hand-drawn look of the style images: drawn by hand on paper with a dark-brown ink pen (#2B2118), an outline about 6 px thick on this canvas, the same weight on every edge, slightly wobbly like a real pen; every area coloured with coloured pencils and wax crayons, the strokes visible as loose diagonal hatching (strokes thinner than the outline), kept inside the outline. Clean and appealing, neither a crude scribble nor overly detailed.
FLAT COLOUR, NO LIGHT, NO SHADOW: every area is one even colour; the pencil and crayon strokes are texture only, they do not model light. No shading, no darker side, no shadows of any kind (no cast shadow, nothing on the background), no white highlights or shine spots, no gloss, no glow, no rim light, no gradients, no 3D look. The only white allowed is one small catchlight dot in each eye, the ivory parts of the design, and the white foam of the water. A darker tone appears only where the design itself has a darker colour.
Plain pure-white background (#FFFFFF): no paper colour or paper grain outside the drawing, no white border or sticker outline around the figure. No text, no labels, no frame, no ground line, no sky, no rocks, no other creature.
