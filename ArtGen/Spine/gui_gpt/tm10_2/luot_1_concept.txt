TASK: design and draw ONE creature as ONE full picture, in the hand-drawn style of images 1 and 2. It is form 2 of a three-form creature line; its final form is image 3.

THE CREATURE: a THREE-HEADED YOUNG SEA DRAGON, an eastern-style water dragon (long serpent necks, no wings, no legs visible), the SECOND form of the creature line whose FINAL form is image 3. It is the same species as image 3 but YOUNGER and SIMPLER: three heads instead of five, and everything a step lighter and plainer. Proportions are those of a young adult, NOT chibi: long snout, brow ridge, sharp almond eyes, small fangs, determined fierce look, neither cute nor scary-horror.
Colours, the same on all three heads and necks: scales jade-green (#45B8A4, clearly LIGHTER and fresher than the deep jade of image 3) on the back and sides; belly plates cream-gold (#F2D9A0) as a row of wide segments along the underside of each neck; a low coral (#E8664A) fin ridge along the back of each neck and a SHORT coral fin mane behind each head (three or four short lobes, much smaller than the big mane of image 3); two STRAIGHT plain golden horns (#E9B84A) per head, smooth spikes curving gently back, NO branches, NO forehead pearl; amber eyes (#F0A030) with a dark slit pupil; inside of the mouth deep rose (#B8405A), teeth cream. Only the CENTRE head has two short cream whiskers (#F4EEDC); the side heads have none. The centre head is the leader: about 20% bigger than the others.

THE WATER, exactly in the style of the water in image 3: stylised hand-drawn waves, plump curling crests like the waves of an old woodblock print but simple and chunky: three flat blues (deep #1E68BE, sky #3A96E2, pale #8CD0F6) in broad bands that follow the flow of each wave, and flat white foam (#F4FCFF) as round bumpy scallops on the crests, all with the same brown ink outline.

CANVAS AND FRAMING: landscape 1536x1024, pure-white background. The whole creature and its water are visible, nothing cut by the edges, at least 60 px of white margin all round; the group is about 1250 px wide and 820 px tall, centred.

COMPOSITION (important):
- The three necks rise out of ONE churning mass of water at the bottom centre and fan out upward. ALL three heads are seen from the side and ALL face the viewer's RIGHT, mouths slightly open, as if watching an enemy on the right.
- The CENTRE head is the highest, its neck an upright S-curve. The LEFT head is a little lower, its neck leaning back to the left and then curving so the head still faces right. The RIGHT head is the lowest, its neck reaching forward to the right. The three heads do NOT overlap each other, each is fully visible.
- A ring of curling waves around the place where the necks come out, lower and fewer than in image 3; one hump of the coiled body arches out of the water at the far left; the coral tail fin rises out of the water at the far right, smaller than in image 3. The front waves overlap the bottoms of the necks so no neck base is seen. A few drops fly above the waves.

DRAWN FOR A CUT-OUT ANIMATION RIG: the pose must be easy to cut into parts: each head clearly separate from the others and from the body with white or water behind it; necks not crossing each other; mouth slightly open so a strip of the rose inner mouth shows; whiskers and fins not covering the eyes or the mouth; the tail fin apart from the body.

LINE AND COLOUR: the hand-drawn look of the style images: drawn by hand on paper with a dark-brown ink pen (#2B2118), an outline about 6 px thick on this canvas, the same weight on every edge, slightly wobbly like a real pen; every area coloured with coloured pencils and wax crayons, the strokes visible as loose diagonal hatching (strokes thinner than the outline), kept inside the outline. Clean and appealing, neither a crude scribble nor overly detailed.
FLAT COLOUR, NO LIGHT, NO SHADOW: every area is one even colour; the pencil and crayon strokes are texture only, they do not model light. No shading, no darker side, no shadows of any kind (no cast shadow, nothing on the background), no white highlights or shine spots, no gloss, no glow, no rim light, no gradients, no 3D look. The only white allowed is one small catchlight dot in each eye, and the white foam of the water. A darker tone appears only where the design itself has a darker colour.
Plain pure-white background (#FFFFFF): no paper colour or paper grain outside the drawing, no white border or sticker outline around the figure. No text, no labels, no frame, no ground line, no sky, no rocks, no other creature.
