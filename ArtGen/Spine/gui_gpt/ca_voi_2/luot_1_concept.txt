CREATURE: a MUTANT WHALE monster of the water element for a monster-collecting game: a small deep-sea whale that mutated: its whole head became one giant fanged maw, its two flippers became clawed legs it stands on, and its tail fluke turned into black sea rock. NO FACE (the key idea of this creature): it has NO eyes, NO eyebrows, NO nose, NO nostrils on the front, NO cheeks, NO expression marks anywhere. Where a head would be there is only ONE ENORMOUS ROUND GAPING MOUTH ringed with big fangs. Do not draw any eye, dot or shape that could be read as an eye, anywhere on the body. Also NO horns, NO ears, NO dorsal fin, NO whiskers, NO tongue. NO white rings, bands or stripes anywhere on the tail or the limbs.
